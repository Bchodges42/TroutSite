## Reach mismatches — deliberately NOT authored (never guessed across reaches)

- clinch-river — the exceptions page's Clinch smallmouth rule covers "Hwy 25E
  to state line": the UPPER Clinch above Norris Lake, a different reach from
  the catalog's Norris-tailwater water (Norris Dam → Kingston mouth). The upper
  Clinch is a separate candidate water (T3-52 family).
- tellico-river — the Tellico smallmouth water is the lake/impounded reach;
  tellico-lake already carries smallmouth-bass (pass 2). The river reach the
  catalog covers is the trout fishery; no river-reach smallmouth rule confirmed.
