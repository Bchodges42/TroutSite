# OWNER POLICY (2026-09-17): "Year Round" is multi-faceted — do not reduce it to holdover proof

Owner directive, given while reviewing habitat-survival batch 1 reads:
"a year round stream doesn't HAVE to mean there were holdovers ... if the river
is always stocked then it's also a year round trout stream. We need to stop
being so black and white."

## The rule

A water qualifies as a year-round trout stream when ANY ONE of these holds —
they are alternative facets, not a checklist:

1. **Survival facet** — documented holdover, wild/self-sustaining population,
   or cold-water mechanism (bottom-draw release, spring source) with data.
   *Sufficient, never required.*
2. **Stocking facet** — a stocking program that runs continuously (all or most
   months, or explicitly owner-confirmed year-round) defines a maintained,
   catchable year-round fishery on its own. TWRA stocking a reach in a month
   is agency operational evidence that trout are catchable there that month.
3. **Angler facet** — the product question is "can anglers consistently catch
   trout here year-round?", not "is survival proven?" Evidence types support
   the angler question; absence of one type does not answer it negatively.

## How this changes reads

- Holdover `documented: false/null` must NOT by itself push a read toward
  unlikely/unknown. It is one facet.
- A continuous stocking program (e.g. obey-river, stocked all 12 months)
  supports `yes` even with zero holdover/wild evidence.
- Conflicting stocking evidence (owner-confirmed year-round vs workbook
  winter-only, e.g. duck-river-tailwater) resolves to `unknown` with the
  conflict stated — not `unlikely` from the older temperature study alone.
- A partial stocking season (e.g. parksville Mar–May) is still meaningful
  negative-side evidence: TWRA's own program is not maintaining that reach
  outside those months. It does not prove absence of trout, but nothing else
  supports year-round either — `unlikely` or `unknown` both stay available,
  chosen by what the rest of the record shows.

## Scope

Applies to: habitat-survival record reads (`overallSurvivalRead`), the
stage-2 enriched classifier's interpretation of `habitatSurvival`, and all
later evidence batches. Records keep the fixed schema; this file carries the
interpretation.
