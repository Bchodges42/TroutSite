Trout accuracy campaign — three fresh-session prompts (usage)

House pattern: docs/EXECUTION-PLAN.md §2. Sessions A/B/C run SIMULTANEOUSLY — each is fully
independent (own clone, own branch, own report), none reads or waits on another. The
implementation plan is NOT written by these sessions: after all three report files exist, the
owner either runs a final planning session (brief available on request) or hands the three
reports to the usual planning model. That planner consumes the three reports plus the repo.

Estimated load (even-split rule, EXECUTION-PLAN §2 — rebalance at handout if any looks >1.5x):
- A ground truth: repo-wide code/data trace + 148-row technical inventory. All three lanes
  (map, scoring, surfaces) are in-repo; heavy on reading and count reproduction.
- B water sources: live verification of every configured gauge ID x parameter, station-catalog
  enumeration, source hunt + recipes for the source/parameter coverage matrix. Heavy on live
  probing; the slowest per item, hence its own session.
- C species & science: per-water species/season evidence hunt + scientific-model review for
  trout and the seven warmwater keys. Heavy on web research and evidence grading.

Lane boundary (stated in both B and C): B owns WHERE measured numbers come from (sources,
stations, parameters, freshness); C owns WHAT the data means for species, seasons, and
scoring science. Temperature crosses both only as: B finds the sensor, C supplies thresholds.

- Branches: campaign-a, campaign-b, campaign-c. Reports (the only files they may write):
  docs/reports/2026-09-13-audit-a-ground-truth.md
  docs/reports/2026-09-13-audit-b-water-sources.md
  docs/reports/2026-09-13-audit-c-species-science.md
