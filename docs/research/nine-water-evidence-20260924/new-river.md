# New River (Scott Co. TN) — trout-evidence research log

Water: New River, Scott County TN (Cumberland basin). Main stem rises in the Anderson Co. coalfield area (upper gauges at Fork Mountain 36.1245,-84.4255 and Braytown 36.1602,-84.3659), courses ~55 mi through Scott/Campbell/Anderson counties (TWRA, citing Evans 1998; drainage 989 km2), and joins Clear Fork at 36.4242,-84.6206 (Scott Co., Oneida South quad) to form the Big South Fork of the Cumberland. The task's guess "formed by Bear Creek and Clear Fork" is NOT correct: Clear Fork is a parallel trunk, not a parent fork; the BSF = New River + Clear Fork. Bear Creek is a tributary (TDEC 0400-40-04 lists Bear Creek in the New River watershed). Direct tributaries documented here: Indian Fork, Smoky (Smokey) Creek, Beech Fork, Buffalo Creek (via Straight Fork), Straight Fork, Jake Branch, Brimstone Creek (enters at New River community, east end — Encyclopedia of Scott Co.), Little Brimstone Cr, Black Wolf Creek (gauges at Glenmary, 36.317,-84.625/-84.654).
Reach discipline: main stem sites labeled upper (Fork Mountain–Braytown–Stainville–Smoky Junction), middle (Norma–Cordell), lower (Hwy 27/New River town to mouth). HUC-8 = 05130104 "South Fork Cumberland" (verified via NWIS + WQP + NHD reachcodes). The task's "HUC 05130107" is WRONG — that is the Collins River (verified: NAS API huc8Name). Clear Fork and BSF main stem kept separate; Rock Creek (Clear Fork trib, Campbell Co.) and BSF/KY reaches noted only as contrast.
Retrieval date for all sources: 2026-09-24. No agency/business/angler contact. No catalog/ledger edits. Research only.

**RECOMMENDATION (bottom line): warmwater-focus.** Every full-community survey that covers this water (Evans 1996 42-locality thesis; TWRA 2004 two-site main-stem IBI; TWRA 2010 Cordell full list; TWRA 2002 Straight Fork 5 sites; TWRA 2010 tributary IBIs) is trout-omitting with complete lists, and TWRA's management actions are explicitly warmwater (1993 smallmouth stocking; musky native-status work; 2004/2007/2010/2013/2016 "Consider winter rainbow trout stocking" = a paper recommendation repeated for 13 years with NO stocking record ever appearing). The single positive trout datum in the entire watershed is one research-grade iNaturalist brook trout (Indian Fork headwaters, 2020-11-07) — a LEAD, not a fishery. The Appalachian Outdoors "seasonal trout / stocked in some sections" claim has no agency support and is now affirmatively contradicted by TWRA's own stocking universe (GIS + JSON + R4 reports).

## E. Contradictions and limits
1. Appalachian Outdoors' "seasonal trout / stocked in some sections" vs TWRA's complete stocking universe (GIS + 2026 JSON + R4 coldwater reports): NO New River destination exists. Claim currently unsupported.
2. TWRA's own 2004-2016 recommendations to "Consider winter rainbow trout stocking" could be (mis)read as evidence of a program; it is a recommendation, never traced to implementation in any TWRA document or dataset found.
3. iNat brook trout (C2) vs all agency surveys: single positive vs ~10 trout-free full lists (1996, 2002, 2004x3, 2010x3, 2006x3, 2011-2017 HCP series). Both stand; the fish is one fish, the surveys are method-documented absence-of-capture in their reaches/seasons.
4. Non-detection ≠ literal absence; surveys are mostly June-September (one late-Sept, one Nov 2006 richness); no winter sampling found anywhere except pond stocking.
5. Name collisions logged: VA New River; KY New River; Blount Co. Little River mislabeled "New River watershed" in TWRA 2010-2014 summary tables; MARIS Oak Ridge brown-trout cluster; HUC 05130107 (Collins) vs 05130104 (correct).

## RECOMMENDATION
**warmwater-focus**, with the Indian Fork brook-trout observation (C2) recorded as an explicit open lead rather than a fishery.
Reasoning: (1) Every method-documented full-community survey of this watershed — Evans 1996 (42 localities), TWRA 2004 (upper + lower main stem, complete lists, 27 C water), TWRA 2010 (Cordell main stem + Smoky Creek + Beech Fork, complete lists, late Sept), TWRA 2002 + 2011-2017 HCP (coldest headwaters) — omits trout, and several are cool-season; (2) TWRA's management record is affirmatively warmwater: 1993 smallmouth stocking, native musky documentation and enhancement, darter/dace conservation; (3) the trout-stocking "consideration" (2004-2016) was never implemented per TWRA's own operational datasets, so "seasonal-stocked" has no supporting record; (4) the sole positive datum is a single, photographed, research-grade brook trout in a tributary headwater (Nov 2020) — under the binding standards that is a LEAD, not a trout fishery, and it does not touch the main stem.
Remaining gap / who holds the settling record: TWRA Region IV (Morristown) — the coldwater program's stocked-waters list and any internal record deciding the fate of the "winter rainbow trout stocking" recommendation (James Habera / Bart Carter line of reports) — plus TVA's Nov 2006 Smoky Creek site-level fish data and NPS BISO STORET uploads. A definitive current answer for the Indian Fork headwaters would require either TWRA stocking archives or a targeted eDNA sample; neither is public today.
