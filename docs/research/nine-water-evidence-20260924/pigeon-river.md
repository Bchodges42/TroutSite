# Pigeon River — TENNESSEE reach (Cocke Co.): trout-classification evidence research log

Water: Pigeon River (main stem), TN reach: NC state line at Waterville → Hartford → Edwina/Berlin → Denton → French Broad River / Douglas Lake head. ~35 km (PRM ~0–23). HUC 06010106.
Research date: 2026-09-24. Research-only pass; no agencies/individuals contacted; no catalog data changed.
Scope discipline: TN main stem only. NC reaches (Waterville Dam tailrace, Canton DH) logged only as contrast.

## Bottom line up front

The TN main stem is TWRA-managed and surveyed as a WARMWATER (black bass / rock bass / IBI) river, with one documented exception: a TWRA experimental SEASONAL RAINBOW TROUT program on the upper gorge reach (Walters Powerhouse → Bluffton, ca. PRM 19–23), Dec 2009–~2015, which TWRA's own reports confirm produced trout catches on the main stem (IBI collections at Denton, July 2010 n=2 and July 2011 n=6) and included TWRA language about "the persistence of wild trout in this section." No trout in any TWRA IBI sample 2012–2017; no Pigeon main-stem point in TWRA's trout-stocking ArcGIS layer (in hand) or in TWRA's published trout stocking schedules 2010–2018; TWRA's Region IV Coldwater Streams report (2020) never mentions the Pigeon. Four coarse brown-trout county records (1997–2004, TWRA data via BISON/MARIS, USGS NAS) sit near the upper gorge/Hartford area but cannot be placed on the main stem.

RECOMMENDATION: **warmwater-focus** (current), with a documented **historic seasonal-stocked** component on the upper reach (2009–ca.2015). Details and reasoning at end.

### G. Physical/temporary regime (context)

## Contradictions and tension points
1. TWRA 2010 report: "persistence of wild trout in this section" vs. complete absence of trout in IBI 2012–2017 and no wild-trout monitoring of the Pigeon in the coldwater program. "Wild" may mean unclipped/holdover fish of stocking origin (incl. NC-origin fish moving over/through Waterville Dam) rather than verified natural reproduction.
2. Experimental stockings 2009–2015 vs. zero main-stem entries in TWRA's own stocking schedules and stocking-location GIS for all years — the program was deliberately "experimental/when available," not a scheduled fishery; it never became official.
3. USGS NAS brown trout 1997–2004 (TWRA-derived) vs. zero trout in TWRA's published Pigeon IBI series of that era (IBI stations PRM 8.2/16.6; 2003 report has no trout). The brown records may sit on ungorged tributaries or be from TWRA datasets not tied to IBI stations; 0.01° precision can't settle it.
4. Summer IBI absence vs. November 8–10.5 °C water: a winter/early-spring seasonal presence is physically plausible and was TWRA's own hypothesis — non-detection in July samples does not refute seasonal presence in the upper reach during the 2009–2015 program, and TWRA's May recaptures (2010) show seasonal survival.

## RECOMMENDATION
**Warmwater-focus** for the current TN main-stem classification, with an explicit documented note that the **upper gorge reach (Walters Powerhouse → Bluffton, ca. PRM 19–23)** carried a TWRA experimental **seasonal rainbow-trout stocking program ca. Dec 2009 – 2015** ("when available"), during which TWRA's own IBI surveys caught rainbow trout on the main stem at Denton (2 in Jul 2010; 6 in Jul 2011) and TWRA noted "persistence of wild trout" in the upper reach; trout were absent from every TWRA IBI sample 2012–2017, from all TWRA stocking schedules 2010–2018, and from the 2020 coldwater report.
Reasoning: (1) 30 years of method-documented agency fish-community data (IBI 1988–2017; CPUE 1997–2016) consistently read as a warmwater black bass/rock bass/walleye fishery — the strongest single body of evidence, both directions considered; (2) the only trout-positive agency evidence is explicitly experimental, seasonal, reach-limited (upper gorge), and terminal (program not continued, no recommendation after 2015, no stocking records thereafter); (3) brown trout 1997–2004 county records are too coarse for reach attribution; (4) all community sources (guides, magazines, forums, YouTube) characterize the TN reach as smallmouth water; the lone community rainbow (Jul 2010 forum lead) falls inside the experimental-stocking window. "Trout" is not supportable; "seasonal-stocked" would overstate a defunct experiment; "unresolved" is unnecessary given the depth of the warmwater record.
