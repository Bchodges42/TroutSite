# Powell River — TENNESSEE main stem (Claiborne & Hancock Co.): trout-evidence research log

Water: Powell River TN main stem — enters TN from Lee Co. VA near Cumberland Gap, flows SW past Arthur / Powell Valley / Tazewell / New Tazewell to Norris Lake (Clinch River system).
Reach discipline: TN main stem only (free-flowing from VA line ~RM 115-121 down to Norris backwater ~RM 54-59, near Arthur). VA reaches excluded (contrast only). TN tributary stockings (Gap/Station/Indian creeks, Richardson "Byrd" Creek) noted only when they touch the main stem.
Retrieval date for all sources: 2026-09-24. No agency/business/angler contact made. No catalog/ledger edits.

**RECOMMENDATION (bottom line): warmwater-focus.** No credible evidence of wild trout or a trout fishery on the TN main stem in any period; the strongest datasets (TWRA's own 1999–2017 main-stem electrofishing series, 1890/1936/1939 museum collections on the main stem, TVA IBI program, TDEC watershed assessments, GBIF/iNaturalist/NAS) are all trout-negative or warmwater-positive. Trout DO occur within ~2 km of the river's head at Cumberland Gap — but on stocked Gap Creek (TWRA tributary program), not the main stem.

## Contradictions and tension points
1. iNat 346568709 (rainbows at Cumberland Gap, 2025) vs. "no trout": resolved geographically — the point is on stocked Gap Creek ~470 m from the main stem, not on the Powell. If a future citation quotes this as "Powell River trout," the correction is the creek identity.
2. onWater/Fishbrain system-level "rainbow/brown trout" for "Powell River" (D3/D4): system-level aggregator entries blur VA (Clinch Mountain/aggregated VA stocking) and TN; the onWater page could not be read (404/403). Discounted for the TN reach.
3. UMMZ 1937 rainbow from Little Sycamore Creek (tributary, TN side) vs. zero main-stem trout 1890/1936/1939: not a contradiction (different waterbodies), but shows occasional trout presence in the TN watershed historically — prevent overclaiming "trout never in the watershed."

## Recommendation
**WARMWATER-FOCUS** — with high confidence for the free-flowing TN main stem (RM 54/59 to VA line), low residual uncertainty at the extreme head (Cumberland Gap), where stocked Gap Creek rainbows exist ~0.5 km from the main channel.
Reasoning: (1) The managing agency's own multi-decade record — TWRA main-stem sportfish series 1999–2017 (8 sites spanning the reach, every 3 years) describing "one of east Tennessee's premier warmwater river resources," with no trout stocking, no trout regulation, and no trout observation ever noted, while explicitly documenting trout programs elsewhere in the same reports; zero main-stem points in TWRA's operational stocking GIS. (2) Museum collections on the main stem from 1890, 1936, and 1939 (96 records, incl. the Bailey survey that underpins Etnier & Starnes) contain zero salmonids while richly documenting the warmwater assemblage. (3) TDEC's watershed assessments/TMDL and TVA's 9-site IBI program (fish-assemblage, "good to excellent") contain no trout anywhere in the TN Powell framework. (4) All modern occurrence databases (GBIF, iNat, NAS) place every trout record either in VA headwaters, in Norris Lake/tailwater, or on stocked tributaries. No evidence of wild trout, no seasonal stocking on the main stem, no historical trout fishery. Remaining gap that could still flip a detail: site-level species lists from TVA's Powell IBI stations (9 sites since 2000) and TWRA's pre-1999 "basic fish data" surveys (Bivens 1988; Carter et al. 2000 [1999 survey], 2003/2004/2006) — holders: TVA stream-monitoring program (contact published in TDEC 2007 plan: Charlie Saylor, TVA) and TWRA Region IV fisheries (Morristown) report archive; plus the unread TVA 1968 "Powell River drainage basin" survey (HathiTrust/Google Books copies exist) for the historical baseline.
