## B. PROGRAM RULES (winter put-and-take)

- Window: stocking begins late November ("TWRA has begun its 2025-26 winter rainbow trout stocking schedule" — release dated 11/26/2025); "continues through February. TWRA's spring stocking begins March 1" (release 12/31/2025). Actual Region-1 pond dates in the 2026 schedule: 1/8/2026–1/15/2026 plus "TBD 12/2026" → one stocking per pond per winter, mid-Dec to mid-Jan in practice. Shelby Farms Park states trout stocked in Jones Pond "every December and January" (fetched). VERDICT: Dec–Feb window (announced late Nov); Nov–Mar is NOT the current TWRA framing.
  - https://www.tn.gov/twra/news/2025/11/26/twra-begins-2025-26-winter-trout-stocking-program.html (fetched 2026-09-09)
  - https://www.tn.gov/twra/news/2025/12/31/2025-26-winter-trout-stocking-program-continues-.html (fetched 2026-09-09)
- Species: Rainbow Trout only, ~10 inches average (both releases).
- Creel: 7/day, no size limit; 5/day at the 5 Community Lakes Fishing Program waters (Cameron Brown Lake Germantown is the only West TN one; others: Cedar Hill Park Pond Madison, Camp Jordan East Ridge, Pistol Creek/Green Belt Lake Maryville, Fountain City Lake Knoxville).
- License: "a trout license is needed in addition to the fishing license" (both releases). (TWRA lake permit is a separate thing and applies to TWRA Family Fishing Lakes, not these city ponds.)
- Put-and-take / summer survival: TN Trout Management Plan 2017-2027 (fetched PDF, https://www.tn.gov/content/dam/tn/twra/documents/fishing/Tennessee-Trout-Management-Plan-2017-2027.pdf): winter trout program "provides trout angling opportunity during the winter months, particularly where there are few or no other trout fisheries… designed to recruit new anglers, especially from urban areas"; "the winter trout program has grown to include 40 locations across the state since 1999… especially in urban areas in Regions 1, 2, and 3"; and "Because survival of stocked trout is usually limited by summer water temperatures, harvest of trout in these waters is generally encouraged." Corroboration (search-only): Camden Chronicle, "TWRA sets winter trout stocking schedule" https://thecamdenchronicle.com/stories/twra-sets-winter-trout-stocking-schedule,37143 — West TN waters "lack the cold-water habitat needed for year-round trout survival."
  → Atlas: yearRound=false for trout at all 13; identity reverts to warmwater the rest of the year.
