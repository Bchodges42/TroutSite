## CROSS-CUTTING FACTS
- Erwin NFH = National Broodstock Program facility (16–20M trout eggs/yr); post-spawn brood fish go to TN tailwaters (TVA/USACE partnerships), Watauga + Nolichucky systems locally, TWRA statewide, and EBCI waters (fws.gov, verified-by-fetch).
- TWRA management plans: "South Holston Tailwater Trout Fishery Management Plan 2022-2027" and "Wilbur Tailwater Trout Fishery Management Plan 2022-2027" (linked from stockings page; PDFs not fetched).
- Tennessee lake trout exist ONLY in South Holston + Watauga reservoirs ("Lake trout … are stocked in South Holston and Watauga reservoirs in the eastern part of the state" — TWRA trout-species page).
- Region 4 reservoir trout reg standard on stocked lakes: 7/day, no size limit, max 2 lake trout.
- Tennessee Trout Management Plan 2017-2027 PDF: /content/dam/tn/twra/documents/fishing/Tennessee-Trout-Management-Plan-2017-2027.pdf (not fetched).
