## PART B — MAJOR FISHERY SPECIES-COMPOSITION CORROBORATION

1. **clinch-river (Norris tailwater)** — rainbow + brown + brook stocked; rainbow-dominated fishery. TWRA official (search-verbatim): "Norris Dam, Clinch River - Brook, Brown, Rainbow - March through August - Special Trout Regulations" [tn.gov/twra/fishing/trout-information-stockings.html] (one outfitter says Mar–Sep [adventureanderson.com/fish-the-clinch/] — TWRA's Mar–Aug is authoritative). VERIFIED BY FETCH (Clinch River TU): "cold tailwater stocked with rainbows, browns and brook trout"; "rainbows comprise 80% to 90% of the catch"; >100,000 fingerlings + a few thousand catchables annually; slot limit since 2008 — release 14–20 in, one >20 in/day, no gear restriction; sulphur hatch early May–late June; wild reproduction present (52% rainbow / 26% brown fingerling survival, 1996 cohort); state-record brown 28 lb 12 oz (late 1980s); rainbows avg 11–12 in, 14–16 in common [crctu.org/fishing.html]. Stripers/walleye below (Melton Hill reach): NOT verified this pass — confidence LOW, needs a TVA/TWRA source. Confidence for species composition: HIGH.

2. **south-holston-river** — wild brown-dominated + wild rainbow; famous sulphur hatch; weir-dam tailwater. "Main attraction is the wild trout and extensive sulphur hatches — two sulphur species" [coastalanglermag.com/south-holston-river/]; ~11,000 brown trout/mile in best sections, 20-in wild RB/BN common [middletennesseeflyfishers.org/south-holston-river.html]; Overmountain TU's partner lodge cites ~8,500 trout/mile [tailwaterslodge.com]; chapter's remit = Watauga/South Holston/Nolichucky watersheds [omtu.org]. TWRA does supplement with fingerlings (standard tailwater practice; not line-item verified this pass). Confidence: HIGH.

3. **watauga-river (Wilbur tailwater)** — rainbow + brown ≈ 50/50 (~5,000 trout/sq mi over 19 mi), brook also stocked; some wild fish; special regs (QTA-type) below Wilbur. TWRA lists "Wilbur Dam, Watauga River" as stocking location (search-verbatim) [tn.gov/twra/fishing/trout-information-stockings.html]; "stocked with rainbow, brook, and brown trout by TWRA, plus a small wild population" [perfectflystore.com/your-streams/fly-fishing-on-the-watauga-river-in-tennessee/]; 50/50 RB/BN, ~5,000/sq mi [thomasandthomas.com/blogs/news/east-tennessee-fly-fishing-south-holston-watauga-rivers]. Confidence: HIGH. ("some brook" claim: supported — TWRA stocks brook here.)

4. **caney-fork-river (Center Hill tailwater)** — CUTTHROAT CONFIRMED. TWRA official (search-verbatim): "Center Hill Dam, Caney Fork River - Rainbow, Brook, Brown, Cutthroat - March through December" [tn.gov/twra/fishing/trout-information-stockings.html]. So the atlas should list 4 stocked species (not just RB+BN), season Mar–Dec. Corroboration: creel regs cover all four species [eregulations.com/tennessee/fishing/trout-regulations]; 15+ miles of trout water below dam [troutzoneanglers.com]. Confidence: HIGH — resolves the open "cutthroat?" question as YES (historically AND currently listed by TWRA).

5. **elk-river (Tims Ford tailwater)** — rainbow + brown (+ brook per one source); put-and-take; 20-in brown reg. TWRA regs (search-verbatim): Elk from Tims Ford Dam to I-65 bridge — brown trout 20-in minimum, 1/day [tn.gov/twra/fishing-regs/trout-regulations.html]; special regs since 2016 [ontheflysouth.com/tims-ford-tailwater-trout/]; stocking incl. 16–21-in browns/rainbows (Mar 17 stocking) [facebook.com/lynchburgtimes]; "brook, brown, and rainbow" stocked [louisianasportsman.com/fishing/freshwater-fishing/out-of-state-destinations-the-elk-river/] + "put-and-take rainbow and brown" [tennesseerivervalleygeotourism.org]. Confidence: HIGH (RB+BN, 20-in brown reg); MEDIUM (brook presence).

6. **hiwassee-river (Appalachia tailwater)** — rainbow + brown (+ some brook); Delayed Harvest Oct 1 – end of Feb (some sources say Mar 1 — TWRA has adjusted dates) from Appalachian Powerhouse to L&N Railroad bridge, single-hook artificials; big-water RB/BN elsewhere year-round. DH Oct–Feb/Mar [southeasternanglers.com/the-rivers/hiwassee-river-fly-fishing-guide-service.html]; DH section + dates [eregulations.com/tennessee/fishing/trout-regulations]; DH start Oct 1 shared with Paint Creek, Tellico [tnvacation.com article]; species RB/BN + some BK stocked by TWRA (search summary). Confidence: HIGH (species, DH existence); MEDIUM (exact DH end date — verify current-year TWRA regs).

7. **obey-river (Dale Hollow tailwater)** — rainbow-primary; TWRA official (search-verbatim): "Dale Hollow Dam, Obey River - Brook, Rainbow - January through December" [tn.gov/twra/fishing/trout-information-stockings.html] — i.e., stocked EVERY month (year-round put-and-take; "rainbow every month" claim in the atlas is right, and brook is also listed). eregulations variant lists RB/BN — minor source conflict; TWRA's own page says Brook+Rainbow. 7.5-mile tailwater to Cumberland confluence [troybassooutdoors.com/2017/03/04/the-obey-river-our-overlooked-tailwater/]; Dale Hollow NFH sits below the dam [onwaterapp.com]. Confidence: HIGH (year-round, rainbow); MEDIUM (brown vs brook secondary species).

## PART C — TENNESSEE TU CHAPTER EVIDENCE
Master list VERIFIED BY FETCH [tctu.org/chapters.html] — eight chapters, east to west (2026-09-09):

1. **Overmountain Chapter** — Tri-Cities (Johnson City/Kingsport/Bristol) — omtu.org — "promotes conservation and restoration for the Watauga, South Holston, & Nolichucky watersheds in Northeast Tennessee" [omtu.org + tu.org/chapters/tennessee/overmountain/]. Waters documented: Watauga R, South Holston R, Nolichucky R (watershed-level, species not itemized on home page). NOTE: the Tri-Cities chapter is Overmountain, NOT "Appalachian."
2. **Cherokee Chapter** — Greeneville/Dandridge/Newport — outflyfishing.org — youth event at Dillard Ponds, Viking Mountain, Cherokee NF (250–300 kids). NOTE: Cherokee is Greeneville-based, NOT Knoxville. Species not itemized.
3. **Little River Chapter** (#644) — Alcoa/Maryville/Townsend/Sevierville — lrctu.org — VERIFIED BY FETCH: GSMNP brook trout restoration/monitoring, Little River + Pistol Creek cleanups, 30+ yr partnership with GSMNP fisheries [lrctu.org]. Calderwood page VERIFIED BY FETCH: 7,015 TN + 7,000 NC rainbow trout stocked in Calderwood; Cheoah/Calderwood/Chilhowee kept cold by Fontana deep water; Brookfield Smoky Mountain Hydropower manages the chain [lrctu.org/?p=1169]. Also spawned Maryville's greenbelt-lake stocking program [maryvillegov.com].
4. **Clinch River Chapter** — Oak Ridge/Anderson Co. — crctu.org — VERIFIED BY FETCH [crctu.org/fishing.html]: rainbows 80–90% of catch, browns+brook stocked, >100k fingerlings/yr, slot limit (14–20 in release) since 2008, sulphur hatch early May–late June, documented wild reproduction. Strongest single species-composition source of any chapter.
5. **Great Smoky Mountain Chapter** (#47) — Knoxville — greatsmokymountain.tu.org — Tennessee's OLDEST chapter; GSMNP focus. (This, not "Cherokee," is the Knoxville chapter.)
6. **Hiwassee Chapter** (#640) — Cleveland/Athens/Reliance — hiwassee.tu.org — VERIFIED BY FETCH: "From the Hiwassee River to the Tellico River and beyond"; Tellico/Citico nav pages; no species itemized on home page.
7. **Appalachian Chapter** (#137) — CHATTANOOGA — appalachian.tu.org — VERIFIED BY FETCH: "From North Chickamauga Creek to the Tellico River and beyond"; no species or stocking claims on home page. (Mission's "Appalachian (Tri-Cities)" mapping was wrong — Appalachian is Chattanooga.)
8. **Music City Chapter** — Nashville — musiccitytu.com — Middle TN waters; Caney Fork access/seasons speaker programs; "Five River Fishing Challenge" [tu.org/chapters/tennessee/music-city/ + tu.myeventscenter.com event listing]. Middle Tennessee Fly Fishers (middletennesseeflyfishers.org) is a separate FFF-style club but publishes species pages for Duck, Caney Fork, Elk, South Holston — useful corroboration.

**MEMPHIS: no active TU chapter.** TCTU (VERIFIED BY FETCH) states the former Delta Chapter served Memphis and the council is seeking volunteers to re-charter [tctu.org/chapters.html]. Memphis Trout Unlimited facebook page exists as a legacy/local group — do not cite as a chapter.
