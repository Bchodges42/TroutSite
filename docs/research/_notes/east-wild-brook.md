## 0. THE CORE DISTINCTION: NATIVE vs STOCKED BROOK TROUT

- The Southern Appalachian brook trout is the **only trout native** to the Smokies and the Southern Appalachians. NPS: "The brook trout is the only native species of trout in the Smokies." [V] https://www.nps.gov/grsm/planyourvisit/fishing.htm (2026-09-09)
- **Every brook trout in East TN park/forest streams is wild and of native southern strain.** TWRA does NOT put hatchery brook trout into Cherokee NF/GSMNP streams; restoration uses wild translocation of native-strain fish. TWRA does stock hatchery brook trout (non-southern, put-and-take) into some **tailwaters** (Clinch, Obey, Hiwassee) — never confuse these with native stream brookies. [V] https://www.tn.gov/twra/fishing/trout-information-stockings.html (2026-09-09)
- GSMNP stopped stocking in 1975; stocking now allowed only for native-species restoration (e.g., smoky madtom). [V] https://www.nps.gov/grsm/planyourvisit/fishing.htm
- Historical decline: logging-era sediment + rainbow/brown introductions (early 1900s; browns ~1950s) pushed brookies into headwaters. [V] https://www.nps.gov/grsm/learn/nature/lynn-camp-prong.htm

### GSMNP restoration program (verified numbers)
- **11 brook trout populations restored, 27.6 stream miles**, park-wide; another **13 miles across 9 streams** have barriers and are future candidates. [V] https://www.nps.gov/learn/nature/lynn-camp-prong.htm → https://www.nps.gov/grsm/learn/nature/lynn-camp-prong.htm
- **Lynn Camp Prong** (Middle Prong Little Pigeon drainage, Tremont): largest project in the SE. Planned 2007; first treatment (8 mi) 2008; final treatment 2011; piscicide Antimycin A + electrofishing removal; setbacks incl. bears and illegal rainbow stocking. Now flourishing; every park stream open to harvest for the first time since park founding. [V] https://www.nps.gov/grsm/learn/nature/lynn-camp-prong.htm
- Program roots: 1978 GSMNP Brook Trout Management Plan. [S] https://npshistory.com/publications/grsm/brook-trout-mgt-plan-1978.pdf
- Recent/ongoing: Moore Springs Branch (NC side, 2.4 mi, EBTJV 2024) [S] https://easternbrooktrout.org/projects/2024-projects/restoring-moore-springs-branch-great-smoky-mountains-national-park-nc ; Indian Flats Prong reintroduction [S] https://landscapepartnership.org/resources/w2b/reintroduce-brook-trout
- 2023: USFWS coordinated moving brook trout FROM GSMNP TO Cherokee NF streams for range-wide recovery. [S] https://www.fws.gov/story/2023-09/restoring-southern-appalachian-brook-trout
- LR TU chapter lists "GSMNP brook trout restoration and monitoring," stream barrier surveys, acid-deposition sampling among activities (no per-stream detail on landing page). [V] https://www.lrctu.org

### Cherokee NF restoration program (USFS + TWRA + TU + TNC)
- USFS official: "Wild trout, rainbow, brown and native brookies are present in most of the mountain streams above 1000 feet in elevation." Stocked rainbows 8–12 in, "typically between March and September." Most popular stocked streams: Tellico River, Citico Creek, Paint Creek, Beaverdam Creek. [V] https://www.fs.usda.gov/r08/cherokee/recreation/opportunities/fishing
- **Sycamore Creek** (Tellico trib, Monroe Co): 1990s restoration (USFS/TWRA/TU rebuilt Pheasant Fields rearing-pool diversion dam); brookies abundant ~2 mi above Tellico confluence, above bedrock falls that block rainbows; lower ~1.8 mi = rainbow; "could become one of the largest streams with southern strain brook trout"; access Trail 163. [V] https://appalachiantu.org/streams/sycamore-creek/ (Herrig, 2007). Featured in TU film "Return of the Southern Appalachian Brook Trout." [S] https://randrflyfishing.com/2015/12/11/return-of-the-southern-appalachian-brook-trout/
- **Trail Fork of Big Creek**: 2021 TU/USFS/TWRA reintroduction — rainbows removed by electrofishing above a natural waterfall; ~2 dozen wild brook trout translocated from nearby creek fall 2021; failing culvert replaced with 50-ft bridge (reopened ~1 mi habitat, protects Del Rio); cost $300k+. [V] https://www.tu.org/magazine/conservation/from-the-field/brookies-in-tennessee-get-a-new-improved-home/
- **Gulf Fork + Wolf Creek (Big Creek drainage)**: hold NATIVE brook trout of a "unique strain of NBT only found in the French Broad Watershed"; Trail Fork population was extirpated (logging) and replaced by stocked rainbows; phase-4 project (2021) replaced Wolf Creek double culvert with stream-simulation open-bottom structure. **EBTJV places this project in COCKE County, Bald Mountains — NOT Greene Co as our atlas currently says. Flag for atlas correction.** [V] https://easternbrooktrout.org/projects/2021-projects/improving-connectivity-for-reintroduced-native-brook-trout-in-trail-fork-of-big-creek-cocke-county-tn
- **North Fork Citico Creek**: brook trout present but scarce, difficult fishing; N Fork + S Fork form Citico Creek. [S] https://appalachiantu.org/streams/north-fork-of-citico-creek/
- TNC/TWRA: "TWRA Fisheries staff have been busy restoring Southern Appalachian Brook Trout... in several Cherokee National Forest streams in the North Zone during recent summer work." [S] https://www.facebook.com/natureconservancytennessee/posts/1457171436439163
- Background: UT thesis, C.G. Romines 2017, "A Predictive Model for Brook Trout Restoration in the Cherokee National Forest." [S] https://voljournals.utk.edu/utk_gradthes/4899/
- Cherokee Chapter TU (cherokeechaptertu.org) funded Trail Fork work via TU TN Council/Cherokee Chapter. [V] (as funders) https://www.tu.org/magazine/conservation/from-the-field/brookies-in-tennessee-get-a-new-improved-home/

### TWRA regulation framework (all verified [V] 2026-09-09)
Source: https://www.tn.gov/twra/fishing-regs/trout-regulations.html
- **Statewide**: 7 trout/day any combination, no length limit (max 2 lake trout). No closed season — trout waters open year-round except specific closures.
- **Designated wild trout streams**: 5 fish/day, no length limit, single-hook artificial lures only, bait prohibited. Full list (East TN): Monroe — North River + tribs, Bald River + tribs, Sycamore Creek + tribs, Rough Ridge Creek + tribs; Carter — Laurel Fork (from cable crossing 0.5 mi upstream of Dennis Cove Rec Area to USFS boundary) + Left Prong of Hampton Creek; Johnson — Beaverdam Creek (Birch Branch confluence → Tank Hollow Rd/USFS 300); Greene — Paint Creek (USFS campground → boundary S of Hwy 70 near Munday Gap); Greene/Unicoi — Rocky Fork (upstream of Rocky Fork Rd & State Park Entrance Rd junction); Little Stony Creek (upstream of Plot Branch confluence).
- **Delayed Harvest (catch-and-release Oct–Feb; artificials only)**: Tellico River (North River mouth → state line) Oct 1–Feb 28; Paint Creek (campground → French Broad River) Oct 1–Feb 28; Doe River (Roan Mountain SP) Oct 1–Feb 28; Hiwassee Oct 1–Feb 28; Big Soddy Creek Nov 1–Feb 28; Buffalo Creek Oct 1–Jan 31; Piney River Nov 1–Feb 28; Acorn Lake Dec 1–Mar 31. (DH ends last day of Feb; then harvest opens — TWRA FB [S].)
- **Tellico-Citico Permit**: required Mar 1–Aug 15 on Tellico River (Turkey Creek confluence → state line) AND Citico Creek; **closed Thursdays & Fridays** in that window; 7 trout/day, no size limit, 1 rod.
- **Horse Creek (Greene Co)**: 7/day, reduced to 2/day May 1–Sept 30.
- **Slickrock Creek** (TN/NC): 4/day, 7-in min, single-hook artificials.
- Stocking schedule updated bi-weekly; "week of" Sundays, within 5 days after. [V] https://www.tn.gov/twra/fishing/trout-information-stockings.html

## 1. GREAT SMOKY MOUNTAINS NATIONAL PARK

**Park-wide rule (applies to every park stream)**: open **year-round**, 30 min before sunrise–30 min after sunset; **5 brook/rainbow/brown trout combined per day**; **7-in minimum**; **single-hook artificials only** (no bait, no treble/double/gang hooks; 2-fly dropper OK); TN or NC license (16+), no trout stamp; all streams open, no closed waters. [V] https://www.nps.gov/grsm/planyourvisit/fishing.htm (2026-09-09). Confidence: **high**.
Species pattern: brown trout lowest elevations → rainbow mid → **brook trout highest headwaters** (only native). ~20% of 2,900 park stream-miles support trout; fish densities 2,000–4,000/mile in good water. [V] fishing.htm + [S] guide corroboration.

- **little-river** — trout-wild — rainbow dominant mid-reaches, brown in slower lower pools (Townsend/Sinks reach), native brook trout in headwater forks; smallmouth lower (TN consumption advisory on Little River smallmouth) — year-round, park regs (5/day, 7-in, single-hook) — [V] https://www.nps.gov/grsm/planyourvisit/fishing.htm + [S] https://www.thetroutbandit.com/post/fly-fishing-for-trout-in-great-smoky-mountains-national-park — confidence: high (regs), medium (species split).
- **leconte-creek** — trout-wild — rainbow + native brook trout in upper reaches — year-round park regs — [V] park page + [S] https://riverreports.com/river-intel/destinations/great-smoky-mountains-national-park — confidence: medium (species).
- **middle-prong-little-pigeon (Tremont)** — trout-wild — rainbow + brown lower; **NATIVE BROOK TROUT RESTORED in Lynn Camp Prong (8+ mi)** — park's flagship restoration, largest in SE — year-round park regs — [V] https://www.nps.gov/grsm/learn/nature/lynn-camp-prong.htm — confidence: **high** (restoration), medium (species split).
- **west-prong-little-pigeon** — trout-wild — rainbow, brook in high headwaters (Walkers Camp Prong brook water per guide), brown toward park boundary/Pigeon Forge — year-round park regs — [V] park regs + [S] https://www.flyfishingsmokymountains.com/planning/where-to-fish-july-august-september/ — confidence: medium.
- **roaring-fork** — trout-wild — rainbow + native brook in upper reach (Roaring Fork Motor Nature Trail); high elevation — year-round park regs — [V] park regs + [S] guide corroboration (flyfisherman.com blue-lining article) — confidence: medium.
- **cosby-creek** — trout-wild — rainbow lower, native brook upper reaches — year-round park regs — [V] park regs + [S] https://riverreports.com/river-intel/destinations/great-smoky-mountains-national-park — confidence: medium.
- **little-pigeon-river (Sevierville reach, OUTSIDE park)** — mixed — **stocked rainbow** (TWRA resumed stocking 2023+: 750 ten-inch rainbows every 2 wks in Pigeon Forge after ~2-decade gap; Gatlinburg stocks its city reach Thursdays with 1,500 TWRA-supplied rainbows post-wildfire; Pittman Center reach stocked); wild fish wash down from park; NO special reg — statewide 7/day, no closed season, stocking typically Mar–Oct (Gatlinburg city permit applies only inside Gatlinburg) — [S] https://www.tn.gov/twra/fishing/trout-information-stockings.html + [S] https://www.wbir.com/article/news/local/twra-resumes-trout-stocking-in-little-pigeon-river/51-418882928 + [S] https://www.gatlinburgtn.gov/page/fishing — confidence: high (stocking), medium (Sevierville city-limits detail).
- **little-tennessee-river (upper, NC/TN)** — mixed — brown + rainbow (wild + NC hatchery-supported stocking on the Needmore/Franklin–Bryson City NC reach; smallmouth too); TN portion is mostly impounded (Calderwood/Chilhowee — both on TWRA's stocked-reservoir list); no brook trout — NC hatchery-supported season: waters stocked and open Mar–early Mar cycle (no closed season; 7 trout/day NC); TN reservoir reaches per TWRA — [S] https://greatsmokiesfishing.com/little-tennessee.html + [V] https://www.tn.gov/twra/fishing/trout-information-stockings.html (Calderwood/Chilhowee listed) — confidence: medium (NC reach detail; low for exact TN free-flowing reach).
