## 7. BROOK TROUT / LAKE TROUT / BROWN TROUT PROGRAMS

Native Brook Trout (Trout Plan 2017-2027) — verified-by-fetch — high:
- Brook Trout is Tennessee's only native trout; historically to 1,600 ft elevation (King 1937).
- Extirpated from 67 of 118 (57%) HUC-12 subwatersheds formerly occupied (EBTJV 2016).
- Now inhabit ~141 miles in 111 streams + one pond (4,000 ft elevation pond); ~25% of TN's wild trout resource outside GSMNP.
- Allopatric (no other trout species) in 40 streams (~46 miles, 32% of resource); 18 more streams have waterfalls/man-made barriers maintaining allopatry over ~38 miles.
- Genetic analyses: over 60% of TN brook populations are of native southern Appalachian heritage.
- Restoration history: TWRA + USFS + Trout Unlimited renovated/enhanced CNF brook populations in the 1980s-1990s by constructing barriers and removing non-native Rainbow Trout; "Ongoing Brook Trout restoration and enhancement projects" continue. ~4% net distribution loss vs previous assessment (Habera et al. 2014).
- Ongoing program corroboration: TWRA Region IV Fisheries Manager Jim Habera gave Southern Appalachian brook trout restoration update at Dec 2023 TFWC meeting — https://preprod.tn.gov/twra/news/2023/12/8/tfwc-concludes-2023.html — 2026-09-09 — search-only — medium; 2012 TWRC release: "The agency has been working toward restoring the native brook trout populations in the Blue Ridge Mountains (Cherokee National Forest)" — https://www.tn.gov/news/2012/3/9/twrc-to-meet-in-nashville-march-15-16.html — search-only — medium.
- Hatchery brook in tailwaters (against mission list): CONFIRMED Norris/Clinch, Apalachia/Hiwassee, Center Hill/Caney Fork, Dale Hollow/Obey, Tims Ford/Elk (plan + live page); PLUS Boone/SFH (live page only). NOT Watauga/Wilbur (Rainbow only per live page). All Brown, Brook, and Lake Trout stocked in TN come from Dale Hollow NFH (DHNFH) — plan — high.
