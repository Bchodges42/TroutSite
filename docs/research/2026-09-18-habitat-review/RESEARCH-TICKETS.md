coldwater rivers flowing into it and its depths. It supposedly has trophy
brown trout. Verify it yourself... If you see what I am talking about we need
to adjust our sources some. If we can find multiple high quality, but non
government sources claiming year round trout... or even an uncovered
government site too."

trophy brown reports from high-quality non-government sources (major outdoor
publications, TU, university), plus any overlooked government page. Multiple
independent quality sources per the evidence rules.

(a) 12-month continuous stocking, OR (b) evidence trout survive long enough
