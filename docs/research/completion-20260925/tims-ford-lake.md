# Tims Ford Lake (Franklin County) — warmwater confirmation log

- Log date / retrieval date: 2026-09-25
- Classification target: warmwater-focus reservoir (trout belong to the sibling Tims Ford tailwater / Elk River water, researched separately)
- Scope note: the RESERVOIR above Tims Ford Dam. The "Tims Ford TW" tailwater rows in trout schedules are a different water.

## 1. Warmwater evidence (dated sources)

**Source 1 — TWRA "Tim's Ford Reservoir" fishing opportunities page** (tn.gov, https://www.tn.gov/twra/fishing/where-to-fish/middle-tennessee-r2/tims-ford-reservoir.html; retrieved 2026-09-25; no on-page date):
- "Tims Ford Reservoir is a 10,600-acre impoundment located in south-central Tennessee with a major portion in Franklin County." "The Tennessee Valley Authority (TVA) owns and operates Tims Ford Reservoir. Dam completion occurred in 1970."
- "The Tims Ford Smallmouth Bass fishery is very popular and considered the best reservoir Smallmouth fishery in middle Tennessee." "The average age of an 18-inch Smallmouth Bass in Tims Ford is 7 years."
- What-you-can-catch list: Black Bass (Largemouth, Smallmouth, Spotted), Crappie (White and Black), Catfish, Striped/Hybrid Striped Bass, White Bass, Yellow Bass, Walleye, Rock Bass, Redear Sunfish, Bluegill/Warmouth — NO trout anywhere on the page (word "trout" absent from body).
- Bass fishing ≈ 75 percent of fishing activity (per TWRA page text surfaced in search, 2026-09-25).

**Source 2 — Bill Dance Signature Lakes (Tennessee Dept. of Tourist Development), Tims Ford Lake page** (https://billdancelakes.tnvacation.com/bill-dance-signature-lakes/lakes/tims-ford-lake; retrieved 2026-09-25; page © 2026):
- Tims Ford is one of 14 Bill Dance Signature Lakes; "optimized and managed for quality bass, striped bass, walleye, and crappie"; enhancements include "Continued stockings of crappie, striped bass, and walleye" and "Annual habitat management and improvements." No trout stockings mentioned.

**Supporting context:**
- TVA (tva.com, retrieved via search 2026-09-25): "Construction of Tims Ford Dam began in 1966 and was completed in 1970"; dam 175 ft high, 1,580 ft across the Elk River; ~10,700-acre / 219,600 acre-ft reservoir.
- Zero-trout-in-lake corroboration: the trout program in this system is the Elk River tailwater below the dam — TWRA trout storymap entry "Tims Ford Tailwater (Elk River)" with 2026 biologist sampling report (March 2026 electrofishing; local captures storymap.html / webmap.json / storymap_item.json, inspected 2026-09-25); PaddleWays elk-river page (searched 2026-09-25): below the dam the river "becomes a cold-water tailwater supporting stocked rainbow and brown trout for approximately 15 miles"; TTU M.S. thesis (Bettoli et al.), "Fate of salmonids stocked into the Tims Ford tailwater, Tennessee" — tailwater-only salmonid research.
- TWRA completed-feed weekly report 2022 (stocking_report_2024.txt-adjacent cw feed, local): "Tims Ford TW" rows only (tailwater), never "Tims Ford Lake".
