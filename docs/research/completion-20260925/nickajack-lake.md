## Identity

- TVA mainstem impoundment of the Tennessee River, impounded 1967 (Nickajack Dam, New Hope/Jasper, TN).
- 10,370 acres at full summer pool, 192 miles of shoreline; southeast Tennessee (Marion County; Tennessee River Gorge reach below Chattanooga). TWRA "Where to Fish," Cumberland Plateau Region 3 (contact 1-931-484-9571).
