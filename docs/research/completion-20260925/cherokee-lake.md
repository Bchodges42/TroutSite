# Research Completion Log — Cherokee Lake (Cherokee Reservoir), Jefferson/Hamblin/Hawkins/Grainger Counties, TN

- Ledger verdict under test: `warmwater-focus` (confirm/strengthen with dated sources)
- Reach scope: Cherokee Reservoir pool — 30,300-ac TVA impoundment (Cherokee Dam completed 1941) on the Holston River; ~393–400 mi shoreline (TWRA page / 2022 Reservoir Report). The Cherokee Dam TAILWATER (Holston River below the dam = the `holston-river` catalog row, resolved to seasonal Nov–Apr by its own completion log) and the Holston main stem upstream of the pool are separate waters, kept separate throughout.
- Research date (retrieval): 2026-09-25
- Mode: internal classification research, owner's map; no agencies/businesses/authors contacted; no edits outside this notes file

## 1. Identity
- Fertile TVA mainstem reservoir; thermally stratifies in summer with oxygen depletion below ~30 ft (drives the striper/walleye summer refuge pattern TWRA describes); winter drawdowns up to 40 ft (TWRA page).

## 4. Contradictions / tension points
1. Tourism/generic fishing sites list "rainbow trout" for the lake (S8) — content-farm boilerplate and a tourism conflation with Mossy Creek; every agency source contradicts. Keep the sibling boundary: the Nov–Apr trout program is the Cherokee TAILWATER (holston-river row), not the lake.
2. Crappie stocking paused in 2022 ("did not receive any supplemental stocking this year"; 2013–2021 mean ~133k/yr) — a program pause, not a fishery change; TWRA's stated management plan: "increased stocking efforts."
3. Sauger: TWRA page says sauger "has a hard time reproducing" in Cherokee; the 2022 survey collected saugeye hybrids and walleye, and stocking tables show saugeye/walleye (not pure sauger) as the maintained fishery — match the ledger's species list accordingly (walleye/saugeye primary; sauger present).
4. "Cherokee bass" is TWRA's regional name for the striper x white bass hybrid (hybrid striped bass) — same fish as the "hybrid" stocking table line; not a distinct trout-adjacent species.
