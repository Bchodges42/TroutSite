# ROCKY RIVER (Van Buren County) — evidence log
Retrieval date for all sources: 2026-09-25. Research only; no agency/business contact.

## 1. IDENTITY

**Verdict (high confidence):** TWRA's "Rocky River" (Region III, Van Buren) is the 31-mile river rising on Jakes Mountain in Sequatchie County (Cumberland Plateau), flowing north through Van Buren County (past Campaign, TN), forming the Warren–Van Buren line, and emptying into the **Caney Fork at Great Falls Lake** near Rock Island (Warren/Van Buren/White tri-junction) — about a mile upstream of Great Falls Dam. It drains ~111 sq mi. It is a Caney Fork tributary (via Great Falls Lake), immediately west of the Cane Creek (Spencer) drainage.

Disambiguation (NOT this water): Rocky Fork (Unicoi Co., scheduled R4); Rocky River rows of other states; "Piney River" Rhea (adjacent R3 water); Rocky Creek/Mill Creek of other regions.

## 4. SPECIES
Rainbow trout only (GIS "rainbow"; all schedule PDFs; 2026 JSON "Rainbow Trout").

## 5. TYPE + CONFIDENCE
- Type: **seasonal spring-stocked stream**, March–May, 3 weekly-window events per year, unbroken 2003–2026. No winter or fall events in any year.
- Confidence: HIGH (24 consecutive primary schedule documents + multiple completed reports; single official GIS site).

## 6. ESTABLISHES VS NOT
- Establishes: identity (Caney Fork/Great Falls Lake tributary via Campaign, Van Buren Co); continuous 2003–2026 March–May stocking; single publicized access (Rocky River Rd bridge, 35.7010, -85.5779); ~1,100 rainbow/site capacity figure (GIS).
- Does NOT establish: any cold-weather stocking; numbers per event per year; brown/brook presence.

## 7. CONTRADICTIONS / CAVEATS
- Watershed grouping differs by source (TDEC groups Rocky River under "Collins River basin"; Wikipedia/GNIS mouth is Caney Fork/Great Falls Lake). Adjacent basins, same Caney Fork system — no impact on stocking verdict.
- 2014/2016 third-mark month (May vs early June) ambiguity is a PDF-glyph artifact; majority of years place three marks cleanly in Mar/Apr/May.
- Web coverage for this small water is thin (Fishbrain/forums absent from results); the verdict rests on TWRA primary documents — appropriate, since schedule rows are the planned-evidence standard.
