# CLEAR CREEK (Morgan County, TN — Obed tributary, NPS-clipped segment)
Research log — internal classification research only. Retrieval date for all sources: 2026-09-25.
Reach: Clear Creek from the Morgan County line (upstream boundary of NPS segment) to its confluence with the Obed River; includes the Rock Creek campground vicinity and the Clear Creek gorge/climbing area. NOT to be confused with: the "Clear Creek" trout site on the Clinch/Norris tailwater (Anderson Co), the Clear Creek in the Big South Fork/New River watershed, or any out-of-state Clear Creek.

### S2. Benck et al. 2017 — NPS Natural Resource Condition Assessment, Obed WSR (NRR-2017/1554)
- Retrieved 2026-09-25: https://npshistory.com/publications/obed/nrr-2017-1554.pdf
- Clear Creek fish passages (fish chapter, p. 145):
  (a) "Clear Creek Watershed — Russ (2006) sampled five locations along Clear Creek within OBRI during the summer of 2005. A total of 24 species were documented, including a variety of darters, sunfish, bass, and shiners... Only one species, the redbreast sunfish (Lepomis auritus), was non-native."
  (b) "During 2005 sampling at two Clear Creek locations, 18 fish species were documented (Scott 2010b)... similar to those found by Russ (2006), with the addition of the blacknose dace (Rhinichthys atratulus)." From the abundance passage: "Scott (2010b) sampled a total of 672 fish at two Clear Creek locations in 2005. The most abundant species were the largescale stoneroller (169 fish), the warpaint shiner (137 fish), and the whitetail shiner (111 fish)."
- Underlying surveys: Russ, W.T. III 2006 (TTU thesis, spotfin chub habitat study, ~4,000 fish/13 sites 2004-2005); Scott, E.M. Jr. 2010a/b (NPS Appalachian Highlands Network fish survey, 2004-2006).
- Also Clear Creek-specific in the NRCA: (c) "In 2002, an oil spill just outside OBRI contaminated the waters of Clear and White Creeks (Trustees 2008)... 'sublethal stress'... was detected in redbreast sunfish and rock bass near the spill site (Trustees 2008, p. 34)... impacts appeared to be short-term, as measures of stress in sampled fish were dramatically lower in 2003 and 2004." Citation: Trustee Council for Resources in the Obed River System. 2008. Damage Assessment and Restoration Plan/EA: Howard/White Unit No. 1 Oil Spill. NPS, Wartburg TN. (d) "Clear Creek within OBRI has been federally designated as critical habitat for the [spotfin chub]."
- "trout" occurrences in full NRCA text: 0. "salmonid": 0. "coldwater": 0.
- Type: NPS condition assessment citing method-documented surveys with counts (5 + 2 sites; 24 + 18 species; 672 fish enumerated to species with abundances) omitting trout. Confidence: high (weighted).
- Establishes: warmwater/minnow-darter fauna with survey-level negative evidence for trout on this reach; the fish-damage species in the spill study (redbreast sunfish, rock bass) are warmwater.

## SPECIES FOUND vs OMITTED (this reach)
- Found: 24 spp. (Russ 2006, 5 sites, summer 2005) and 18 spp. (Scott 2010b, 2 sites, 2005; 672 fish; top = largescale stoneroller, warpaint shiner, whitetail shiner; + blacknose dace); darters/sunfish/bass/shiners; non-native redbreast sunfish. Park-level sportfish incl. smallmouth bass.
- Omitted: all Salmonidae.

## VERDICT
Warmwater-focus CONFIRMED. Two method-documented dated surveys (2005, NPS/TTU) enumerate the fish community on this exact reach with zero salmonids; NPS markets the reach as smallmouth water; TWRA stocks no trout here (its "Clear Creek" trout site is the Clinch/Norris tailwater access); spotfin chub critical habitat designation corroborates a warm, clear, upland-warmwater character. Gaps: no post-2006 agency survey located for this reach alone; the 2020 brook-trout iNat pin is unresolved (drainage-ambiguous single human observation).
