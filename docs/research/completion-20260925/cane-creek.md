# CANE CREEK (Bledsoe / Van Buren counties) — evidence log
Retrieval date for all sources: 2026-09-25. Research only; no agency/business contact.

## 1. IDENTITY (first deliverable)

**Verdict (high confidence):** The ledger row "Cane Creek (Bledsoe/Van Buren)" is the single stream that rises in Bledsoe County (Little Mountain area, near the Sequatchie Valley rim), flows north through **Fall Creek Falls State Park** (park straddles the Bledsoe/Van Buren line; Cane Creek Gorge / Cane Creek Falls / Cane Creek Cascades), then past **Spencer, Van Buren County**, to the Caney Fork–Collins River system near Rock Island. TWRA has managed it as ONE schedule destination ("Cane Creek", Region III, Van Buren) 2003–2018, then SPLIT it into two destinations from the 2019 schedule onward:
- **"Cane Creek (Fall Creek Falls State Park)"** = upper park reach (TDEC-managed; GIS "Upper Cane Creek" sites).
- **"Cane Creek (lower)"** = downstream private-land reach near Spencer.

Disambiguation (NOT this water): Cane Creek, Perry Co (Region 1, scheduled 2003–2026); Cane Creek, Hickman Co (Region 2); Cane Creek Park lake, Putnam Co (winter program); Cane Creek, Montgomery Co (2017 sheet); Cane Creek, Morgan Co (TWRA access sheet "CaneCreek.pdf" shows Hwy 331/Beardstown — different basin).

- Confidence: months = HIGH (24 consecutive years of primary schedule documents + completed reports); identity = HIGH; 2018 park-launch details = MEDIUM.

## 6. ESTABLISHES VS NOT
- Establishes: continuous seasonal stocking 2003–2026; March–May core on the lower reach; Feb/Mar–May + Oct on the FCF park reach from 2019; identity of the two modern TWRA destination names and their coordinates.
- Does NOT establish: monthly precision before 2019 for the park reach separately (row was consolidated); brook trout presence (secondary only); any 2026 completion of the lower reach (no completed record retrieved).

## 7. CONTRADICTIONS / CAVEATS
- Wikipedia (FCF article) says Cane Creek "empties into the Caney Fork River"; TDEC/Wikipedia basin grouping elsewhere puts the lower reach in the Collins basin grouping. Either way it is the Upper Caney Fork system; does not affect the stocking verdict.
- 2014/2016 third-mark month ambiguity (May vs early June) is a PDF-glyph alignment artifact, not a source conflict.
- The 2024 completed feed lists "Cane Creek" without "(lower)" — same water (Region 3, Van Buren); do not confuse with Region 1 Perry "Cane Creek" (04/30/2024) in the same feeds.

## 9. RECOMMENDATION
Classify **Cane Creek (Bledsoe/Van Buren — Fall Creek Falls STP / Spencer)** as a **seasonal (spring) stocked stream**: lower reach stocked **March, April, May** (3 weekly-window events) every year 2003–2026; park reach (TWRA "Cane Creek (Fall Creek Falls State Park)") stocked **late February–May plus a single late-October event** since 2019. Keep the ledger row split-aware: 2003–2018 = one row; 2019+ = "Cane Creek (lower)" + "Cane Creek (Fall Creek Falls State Park)". Exclude Perry/Hickman/Putnam/Morgan/Montgomery Cane Creeks from this row.
