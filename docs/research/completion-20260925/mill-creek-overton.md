# MILL CREEK (Overton County — Standing Stone State Park) — evidence log
Retrieval date for all sources: 2026-09-25. Research only; no agency/business contact.

## 1. IDENTITY

**Verdict (high confidence):** TWRA's Overton County water is scheduled, from 2022 onward, as **"Mill Creek (Standing Stone State Park)"** (Region III). Mill Creek rises on Reynolds Mountain near Allons (Overton Co), flows west/northwest through Standing Stone State Park (Hilham/Allons, Overton Co), where Standing Stone Dam impounds it into 69-acre Standing Stone Lake (joined there by Morgan Creek and Bryans Fork), then continues to the **Obey River / Cumberland drainage** — i.e., NOT the Caney Fork basin; flag this in the ledger. The stocked sites sit on the creek/lake inside the park (~36.471, -85.418).

Disambiguation — the Region 2 oddity resolved (HIGH confidence): TWRA runs a DIFFERENT, long-standing "Mill Creek" in **Hickman County** (Region 2, Wrigley area; TWRA access sheet Mill_Creek.pdf shows Hwy 48/Big Spring Creek/Lyles — Hickman). The 2024 completed feed's row "Region 2 Mill Creek 05/15/2024" is the **Hickman** water (it matches cw2024may "2 Mill Creek 05/15/2024"), NOT the Overton park water. Other same-named waters in old schedules: Mill Creek, Lawrence Co (2003–2007); Mill Creek, Macon Co (2003, 2008–2010); Mill Creek, Cumberland Co (2017). None is this water.

## 7. CONTRADICTIONS / CAVEATS

The 2024 completed-feed snapshot does NOT document an Overton completion; the 05/01/2024 completion comes from the May-updated coldwater schedule.
- Drainage: ledger groups this under "Caney Fork basin" — actually Obey/Cumberland drainage. Keep the row but correct the basin note.
- 2025 third mark: read as March + April with a possible late-February event; 2026 JSON (3/22, 4/26) supports a two-event spring norm.
