# Wolf River (West TN — Memphis/Shelby/Fayette) — warmwater classification log

Retrieval date for all web sources: 2026-09-25 unless noted. Research only; no agency/business/author contact.

Water: the WEST TENNESSEE Wolf River (~100 mi; rises at Bakers Pond, Benton Co. MS; flows northwest through Fayette and Shelby counties to the Mississippi at Memphis). NOT the Cumberland-plateau Wolf River of Fentress County (which receives the TWRA trout stockings in the 2026 schedule and has the smallmouth PLR exception "South Ford Road Bridge downstream to Dale Hollow Reservoir" on the same eRegulations page). Lower 22 miles channelized by USACE by 1964; middle reaches dredged in the late 1970s to Moscow; upper/Ghost reaches unchannelized.

## Warmwater / fishery evidence

1. **Wolf River Conservancy — "The Wolf River" page** — https://www.wolfriver.org/explore/the-wolf-river (fetched 2026-09-25; page undated; org founded 1985). Fields: the Wolf is "spring-fed"; Ghost River section "an area that loses its banks and meanders in countless channels through a cypress-tupelo forest"; portions of the river are listed as **Exceptional Waters of Tennessee**; channelization history (USACE West Tennessee Tributaries Project, late 1940s; "By 1964, the Corps of Engineers had channelized the lower 22 miles"; late-1970s dredging to Moscow); "Only the Wolf River and its big sister, the Hatchie River, remain largely unchannelized" (WRC framing among West TN systems); wetlands recharge the Memphis Sand Aquifer; WRC has protected 20,000+ acres incl. Ghost River State Natural Area. **No fish species list on the page.** Warmwater evidence: indirect (warm lowland swamp/oxbow system; Exceptional Waters). Type: watershed conservancy page. Confidence: MEDIUM-HIGH for river character; silent on fish.

2. **TWRA — "Wolf River WMA (and Ghost River SNA)" page** — https://www.tn.gov/twra/wildlife-management-areas/west-tennessee-r1/wolf-river-wma-ghost-river-sna.html (fetched 2026-09-25; page reflects 2026–2027 season dates). Fields: Wolf River WMA 4,236 acres (2,400 in the Wolf River floodplain; >80% bottomland hardwood/swamp forest); Ghost River SNA "a 2,220-acre, 14-mile section of the Wolf River from the parking area near La Grange to the Bateman Bridge"; canoe put-in ramp at La Grange, take-out at Bateman Rd, blazed canoe trail with 600-ft boardwalk in Minnow Slough. No fish list. Warmwater evidence: indirect — TWRA's own managed paddling/boating corridor on the unchannelized Wolf (agency presence + documented boat access). Confidence: HIGH for what it states.

3. **TDEC — "Ghost River State Natural Area" page** — https://www.tn.gov/environment/natural-areas/what-we-do/sna/cp/ghost-river.html (fetched 2026-09-25; SNA designated 1997; boardwalk completed 2004). Fields: "The Ghost River is a 2,306-acre section of the Wolf River in Fayette County"; "an unchannelized river section that meanders through bottomland hardwood forests, cypress-tupelo swamps, and open marshes"; braided channels/backwater sloughs "provide excellent habitat for rare aquatic organisms including endangered freshwater mussels and fish" — rare FISH named: **northern madtom (Noturus stigmosus)**; 22+ mussel species; "fishing is permitted at the site." Warmwater evidence: YES (native warmwater/southern fauna; recreational fishing sanctioned). Type: state agency natural-area page. Confidence: HIGH for what it states.

4. **TWRA regulations via eRegulations — "Exceptions to Statewide Regulations," Last Updated: September 15, 2026.** https://www.eregulations.com/tennessee/fishing/exceptions-to-statewide-regulations (opened 2026-09-25). Verbatim: "Forked Deer, Hatchie, Loosahatchie, Obion and Wolf Rivers (includes tributaries)" — "Crappie: 30 per day, no length limit." **Crappie-rule confirmation:** the West TN Wolf is a named member of the West TN rivers crappie exception (the river group is unambiguously the West TN set: Forked Deer/Hatchie/Loosahatchie/Obion/Wolf; the Fentress County Wolf's separate smallmouth PLR entry explicitly runs to Dale Hollow Reservoir, i.e. the other Wolf). Type: current agency regulation (dated Sept 15, 2026). Confidence: HIGH.

5. **PiscaMaps, "Wolf River" page** — https://piscamaps.com/us/tennessee/rivers/wolf-river (fetched 2026-09-25; "Rules verified 2026-09-01 against TWRA regulations"). Fields: smallmouth/largemouth/spotted bass, black and white crappie, bluegill, channel catfish, walleye, common carp — **and "Rainbow Trout."** TREATMENT: the rainbow-trout entry is treated as app-list NOISE / single-catch LEAD, contradicted by all agency datasets (below): the 2026 schedule has zero Wolf rows in Shelby/Fayette (Shelby trout = four city Winter-program ponds only), NAS Shelby+Fayette zero, GBIF corridor zero. Smallmouth and walleye are likewise implausible/noise for the lowland Wolf (not documented by any agency source found). Warmwater evidence: YES for the bass/crappie/panfish/catfish core. Type: angler app/database. Confidence: LOW-MEDIUM (LEAD only).

6. **The Tennessee Conservationist (TDEC magazine), May/June 2025, "Wolf River Conservancy," by Ryan Hall** — https://digital.tnconservationist.org/articles/wolf-river-conservancy (issue contents + partial article text retrieved via mirror 2026-09-25). Fields: WRC as an accredited land trust, "one of the most effective conservation organizations in the region," 20,000+ acres protected; WRC volunteer river-guide paddle program active monthly. Dated 2025 state-magazine feature documenting the Wolf's conservation/paddling prominence. Type: dated state publication. Confidence: MEDIUM (full text paywalled/flipbook; partial retrieval).

Dated first-person kayak-fishing account of the Ghost section. Warmwater evidence: LEAD only. Confidence: LOW.

## Verdict / recommendation
**Warmwater CONFIRMED.** The West TN Wolf is a TWRA-regulated warmwater river: named member of the current (2026-09-15) West TN crappie-exception list; crappie/bass/panfish/catfish-class angler databases (with a flagged rainbow-trout app-noise lead contradicted by every agency dataset); TWRA-managed Wolf River WMA with La Grange–Bateman paddling corridor; TDEC-documented unchannelized Ghost section with native warmwater rare fauna (northern madtom, 22 mussels) and sanctioned fishing; Exceptional Tennessee Waters reaches. **Zero trout evidence of any category:** no schedule row (West TN Wolf), no NAS record, no GBIF record, no wild iNat record (the only corridor hits are captive Bass Pro Pyramid aquarium fish), no trout on any agency page; Memphis-area trout exist only in stocked city ponds (Cameron Brown, Edmund-Orgill, Johnson Park, Shelby Farms) and a captive aquarium. Suggested label: "warmwater (documented bass/crappie/panfish fishery; Ghost River wild section; no trout evidence, any category)."

Gaps: no TWRA electrofishing/creel report for the Wolf located this pass; striped-bass stocking lane unresolved (rate limits; trout-irrelevant); Fishbrain Wolf page not opened (rate limits) — PiscaMaps + TNDeer carry the angler-log lane.
