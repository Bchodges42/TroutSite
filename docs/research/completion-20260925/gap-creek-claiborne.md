## Species
Rainbow Trout only (2026 JSON SPECIES; GIS rows rainbow).

## Type + confidence
- Type: Seasonal put-and-take, three trips per year, mid-Feb / mid-Mar / mid-Apr (current regime). TYPE field "Seasonal".
- Confidence: HIGH 2018–2026; MEDIUM 2010–2013; NONE 2003–2009, 2016–2017; 2014–2015 explicitly empty rows (no stocking scheduled).

## Establishes vs not
- Establishes: no December or January stocking in any located year; February begins the season (mid-Feb since 2018); spring-only 3-trip regime 2018–2026.
- Does not establish: 2003–2009 months; 2016–2017; completed-feed confirmation for Feb weeks (located completed reports only date Mar/Apr feedings).

## Holdover / reproduction
None documented. Gap Creek (Claiborne) does not appear in Region IV wild-trout sampling tables (Region IV Coldwater Streams Reports 2018–2023 — zero hits for the Harrogate trio). No monitoring, no creel, no reproduction records. Put-and-take only; small private-land stream with marginal summer flow.
