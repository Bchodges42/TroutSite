## Type + confidence
- Type: Seasonal put-and-take stream stocking, Feb–Jun era (2018–present); earlier Mar/Apr + Jul–Sep era (2010–2013). TYPE field = "Seasonal".
- Confidence: HIGH for 2018–2026 (mutually consistent PDF grids + JSON + completed feeds). MEDIUM for 2010–2015 (layout-decoded grids). NONE for 2003–2009, 2016–2017 (evidence gap).

## Establishes vs not
- Establishes: planned and completed stocking occur ONLY March–June (2018–2026); July–September in 2010–2013 era; zero planned or observed December–February stockings in any located source.
- Does not establish: 2003–2009 months; 2016–2017; whether the 2014 skipped row reflected an actual lapse (no completed-feed archive for 2014–2015 located).

## Holdover / reproduction
None documented. Horse Creek proper is absent from TWRA Region IV wild-trout sampling tables except one 1994 quantitative sample (Region IV Coldwater Streams Report 2018, Table A-1: "Horse Creek, Nolichucky, Greene, CNF, 1994, RBT" — i.e., CNF headwater reach, not the stocked lower reach). No reproduction/holdover studies; Region IV 2023 report describes these stocked streams as limited by "marginal summer habitat" — classic put-and-take, fish out within weeks. No creel or electrofishing record for the stocked reach located.

## Contradictions
1. Catalog months [12,1,2] (Dec/Jan/Feb) contradict every located schedule year (2010–2013, 2018–2026) and every completed feed (2022–2025). KILL [12,1,2].
2. Nov–Dec 2024 and Aug–Sep 2025 completed reports actively list other Region 4 waters while Horse Creek is absent — direct negative evidence against Dec–Feb stocking.
3. Horse Creek does not appear in the 2017–18 winter trout program document (winter_trout_2018.pdf, 0 hits).
4. Special regulation (2-trout limit May 1–Sep 30 on USFS reach) is consistent with a warm-season wild-fish protection window, not with Dec–Feb stocking.
