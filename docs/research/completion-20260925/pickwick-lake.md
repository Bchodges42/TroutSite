## Identity

- TVA mainstem impoundment of the Tennessee River (Pickwick Dam, 1938): 43,100 acres total across TN/AL/MS, ~6,159 acres in Tennessee, entirely within HARDIN COUNTY on the TN side; max depth 85 ft; embayments incl. Yellow Creek (partial), Winn Springs, Dry Creek, Pickwick Landing State Park; tri-state reciprocal regs from the dam upstream to TRM 224.8. TWRA "Where to Fish," West Tennessee Region 1.
