# Lake Barkley (Barkley Reservoir) — Stewart/Montgomery/Dickson counties TN (+ Lyon/Trigg KY) — Cumberland River impoundment

Research date: 2026-09-25 (retrieval date for all live fetches; Wayback captures dated individually).
Ledger verdict under test: `warmwater-focus`. 190-water completion sweep, final three.

## Identity
- Barkley Reservoir ("Lake Barkley"), impoundment of the Cumberland River below Dale Hollow/Ky. line, built by TVA (Kentucky Dam = Barkley Dam, completed 1966). TN-side shore: Stewart, Montgomery, Dickson counties; KY-side: Lyon/Trigg. TN portion managed by TWRA (Where-to-Fish places it in **Region 1 / West Tennessee**); KY portion and the tailwater below Barkley Dam managed by KDFWR. Barkley WMA (TWRA) adjoins in Stewart Co. (Yellow Creek Access known — not re-chased).
- Coordinates (approx. TN mid-lake, Bumpus Mills area): 36.50 N, -87.86 W (not contested in ledger).
