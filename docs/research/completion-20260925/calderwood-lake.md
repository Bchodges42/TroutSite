### 2. TWRA Trout Management Plan 2017–2027 (Fisheries Report No. 17-10, ed. James W. Habera, October 2017)

- Retrieval: 2026-09-25 (PDF downloaded; pdftotext).

- Verbatim (reservoir program): "Only reservoirs that maintain a year-round supply of cold, well-oxygenated water can support trout fisheries. Tennessee has nine reservoirs that currently support trout fisheries: Dale Hollow, Parksville, South Holston, Wilbur, Watauga, Fort Patrick Henry, Calderwood, Chilhowee, and Tellico (~62,400 acres total). Some reservoir trout attempt to spawn in tributaries, but these attempts are largely unsuccessful and stocking is required to maintain reservoir fisheries. Trout are stocked during the winter to assure that surface water temperatures are cold enough for their survival."

- Type/confidence: agency management plan; HIGH.

## Stocking season / calendar (synthesis)
- TWRA (TN): Nov + Dec (2026 schedule months "N, D"); Dec 2024 event archived. Winter-only by design (TMP: winter stocking "to assure that surface water temperatures are cold enough").
- NCWRC (NC share): spring entries Apr (and Jun) on the 2026 District 9 schedule (column reading medium-confidence; a master-list snippet indicates Oct–Dec entries too).
- Species: Rainbow Trout only, both programs, 2026. No brown/brook/laker stocking documented at Calderwood.
- No month in which the water is fishless by design: statewide trout season has no closed season; fish carry over in deep cold habitat (TMP describes summer deep-water angling; TWRA page: "thrive in the cold, clear water").

## Year-round verdict analysis
- Criterion (TMP, verbatim above): Calderwood is on the nine-reservoir list defined by "a year-round supply of cold, well-oxygenated water," and "stocking is required to maintain" the fishery. Agency statements: annual stocking + year-round cold habitat + no closed season + documented summer deep-water fishery (0.6 trout/h creel, 1999).
- What is NOT present: continuous/12-month stocking (2026 months are N, D only).
- Standard applied: "year-round requires continuous stocking OR documented holdover/refuge with an agency/utility statement." The holdover mechanism is the TMP-documented year-round cold, well-oxygenated habitat plus agency statements of annual stocking and summer deep-water catchability; the private-utility (APGI) cost-share makes the program a FERC license obligation through 2045.
- Recommendation: KEEP `year-round-trout`. If the ledger supports months annotations, pin stocking months "N, D" (TWRA) with "Apr/Jun (NCWRC, NC share)" as secondary; do NOT pin as the fishery's presence months, which are year-round via holdover. Confidence: moderate-high (agency criterion + annual-stocking statements + multi-year schedule), not absolute (no directly retrieved TWRA statement using the exact words "year-round" for Calderwood except via WATE paraphrase and the snippet-level TNSOS proclamation).
