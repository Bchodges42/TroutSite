# Obion River (main stem, West TN) — warmwater classification log

Retrieval date for all web sources: 2026-09-25 unless noted. Research only; no agency/business/author contact.

Water: Obion River MAIN STEM (from the Weakley–Obion County-line convergence of the North/Middle/South/Rutherford Forks, through Obion County past Kenton/Rives/Obion/Troy, to the Obion–Forked Deer merge ~3 mi from the Mississippi). Distinct from the four forks mined in the low-confidence pass; this log covers the main stem. Warmwater-focus; 2019 levee-failure flood coverage and Reelfoot-adjacent ponds excluded from the fishery case.

## Warmwater / fishery evidence

1. **Dickinson, William Clark, "The Fishes of the Obion River System," M.S. thesis, University of Tennessee, Knoxville, Aug 1973** (advisor D.A. Etnier, 91 pp; TRACE https://trace.tennessee.edu/utk_gradthes/8225; local full text low-confidence/fwr_data/obion/dickinson1973.txt, mined 2026-09-25). MAIN-STEM content:
   - Geography (verbatim): "The convergence of these forks near the Weakley-Obion County line forms the main river, which then continues through a valley about 50 miles long and merges with the Forked Deer River about three miles east of the Mississippi River."
   - Appendix station block (verbatim): "Numbers 171 through 183 -- Main Obion River" — 13 dedicated main-stem collections (survey window fall 1971–summer 1973; seine/gill/hoop-net/rotenone-methodology system-wide survey, 183 collections total).
   - Dated state surveys OF THE MAIN CHANNEL cited in the species accounts: "A single specimen of threadfin shad was reported in a 1972 Game and Fish Commission survey from the main river in Obion County"; "Three specimens of quillback were reported in a 1972 Game and Fish Commission survey from the main channel of the Obion River in Obion County"; (prior-pass extraction) "Four specimens of blue catfish were reported in a 1961 Game and Fish survey" of the old channel; river shiner "recorded from a single locality in the lower portion of the main river."
   - **Zero trout: "trout"=0, "Salmonidae"=0, "Salvelinus"=0, "Oncorhynchus"=0 across all 91 pages; the only two "Salmo" strings are substrings of Micropterus salmoides (largemouth bass) — verified 2026-09-25.**
   Warmwater evidence: YES (main-stem collections + dated 1961/1972 state surveys of the main channel; warmwater assemblage incl. catfish, crappie, bass, buffalo, gar, drum). Type: academic systematic survey, museum-vouchered. Confidence: HIGH.

2. **TWRA 2026 regulations via eRegulations — "Exceptions to Statewide Regulations," Last Updated: September 15, 2026.** https://www.eregulations.com/tennessee/fishing/exceptions-to-statewide-regulations (page opened 2026-09-25)
   Fields (verbatim): "Forked Deer, Hatchie, Loosahatchie, Obion and Wolf Rivers (includes tributaries)" — "Crappie: 30 per day, no length limit."
   **Crappie-rule confirmation:** the Obion (main stem included by name; "includes tributaries" covers the forks) is a named member of TWRA's West TN rivers crappie exception — a warmwater gamefish regulation on the river itself. Type: current agency regulation (dated Sept 15, 2026). Confidence: HIGH.

3. **PiscaMaps, "Obion River" (main stem) page** — https://piscamaps.com/us/tennessee/rivers/obion-river (fetched 2026-09-25; page states access rules "verified 2026-09-01 against TWRA regulations").
   Fields: species listed — largemouth bass, spotted bass, black crappie, white crappie, bluegill, channel catfish, common carp (+ smallmouth bass, walleye: implausible for a channelized West TN lowland main stem and absent from Dickinson 1973 — app-list noise, NOT treated as evidence). **Trout: not listed.** Warmwater evidence: YES (angler-database corroboration). Type: angler app/database. Confidence: LOW-MEDIUM (LEAD only).

## Verdict / recommendation
**Warmwater CONFIRMED (main stem).** Anchors: 13 main-stem collections in Dickinson 1973 plus dated 1961/1972 Game and Fish Commission surveys of the main channel; the Obion's named membership in the current (2026-09-15) TWRA crappie-exception waters list; angler-database main-stem pages (largemouth/crappie/catfish/drum, no trout); Rivergator's documented lower-main-stem paddling reach. Zero trout in any category (616-row 2026 schedule, NAS 7-county, GBIF basin box, 1973 full-system survey — all with controls). Suggested label: "warmwater (documented bass/crappie/catfish-class fishery; 1973 systematic survey; no trout evidence, any category)."

Gaps: no post-1973 published main-stem fish survey with methods found (TDEC lane is benthic/macroinvertebrate-focused); no dedicated main-stem press float story located distinct from the 2012 North Fork account; tournament/guide lane rate-limited early (low prior probability of trout relevance).
