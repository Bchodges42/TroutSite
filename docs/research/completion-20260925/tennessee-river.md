## Identity

- The main-stem Tennessee River run through Hardin County: the "tennessee-river" ledger row sits in Hardin County near Pickwick Dam/Shiloh. Above the dam the mainstem is Pickwick Reservoir; below Pickwick Dam (Hardin Co., TRM ~206.7) the mainstem continues as the Pickwick tailwater, crossing into Alabama within a few river miles. The reach is a warmwater corridor whose signature runs are the winter SAUGER run below the dam and migratory bass/catfish movement — there is no TWRA standalone "Tennessee River" Where-to-Fish page for West TN (verified against the r1 index); the agency documents this reach through its reservoir pages, record-fish locations, and regulations.
