# Doe River (Carter County, TN) — Evidence Log

Retrieval date: 2026-09-25 (all retrievals this date unless noted). Research only; no contact with any agency/author.

## Identity
Doe River, Carter County: rises on Iron Mountain, flows through Roan Mountain State Park (RMSP) and the town of Roan Mountain, then Hampton, and joins the Watauga River at Elizabethton. Region 4. Two stocking strata exist in TWRA's own data: "Doe River (Up)" (Roan Mountain/RMSP) and "Doe River (Lw)" (Hampton–Elizabethton). Distinct from Doe Creek (Johnson County, Watauga Reservoir tributary) — always separated by county column.

### S9. TWRA Region IV Coldwater Trout Report 2018 (Doe River chapter)
- Org: TWRA Region 4. Wayback: https://web.archive.org/web/20220804000418/http://www.tn.gov/content/dam/tn/twra/documents/fishing//trout/Coldwater-Trout-Report-R4-2018.pdf. Retrieved 2026-09-25.
- Key statements:
  - "Between Cove Creek and the town of Roan Mountain, Doe River supports **some of Tennessee's finest populations of wild Rainbow and Brown trout**."
  - "TWRA continues to maintain a **put-and-take fishery for stocked Rainbow Trout** in the Doe River, primarily to help meet angler demand in the Roan Mountain State Park vicinity. **About 8,500 catchable Rainbow Trout are stocked in this area each year during March-June.**"
  - "**A new Delayed Harvest fishery (November through last day of February) was established in Doe River within Roan Mountain State Park in 2018. A total of 1,625 Rainbow Trout were stocked for this fishery during fall 2018 and January 2019.**" ← the only winter (January) stocking evidence found for this water, one-time at DH inception.
  - Monitoring station downstream of RMSP: 36.18243 N, 82.07546 W (sampled 28 Aug 2018); wild Rainbow + Brown trout with balanced size structure; whirling-disease pathogen (M. cerebralis) detected 2018, no clinical disease.
  - 2003 roving creel: highest trout angler effort (459 h/ha) and total catch among five Region 4 study streams.
  - Management: "Doe River supports an outstanding fishery for wild Rainbow and Brown Trout that future management should emphasize."
  - TU/City of Roan Mountain proposed additional Mar–May stocking on a currently unstocked reach between Hwy 19E (36.205815, -82.105594) and Holly Hill Rd (36.230776, -82.144299) — a proposal only.
- Establishes: Mar–Jun program scale (8,500/yr); DH creation (2018, RMSP, Nov–Feb C&R, stocked fall + Jan 2019); agency-documented wild reproduction.

## Contradictions
1. **Ledger "YR flag + months [12,1,2]"**: the Dec–Feb window matches NOTHING in the stocking record. It most plausibly derives from the RMSP Delayed Harvest C&R season (Oct 1–Feb 28) being mistaken for a stocking window. TWRA's statewide winter program has never included the Doe; the only winter stocking ever documented is the one-time January 2019 DH stocking at DH creation.
2. **ArcGIS layer** shows all 24 Doe sites as "Spring"/no-DH while the schedule JSON and regs show a fall DH event and DH designation — TWRA's own fields are internally inconsistent; the schedule + regs + R4 report (three sources) outweigh the layer's blank tag.
3. "Stocked March to October" phrasing (stockingmap) = Mar–Jun + Oct event, not continuous Mar–Oct stocking.

## Recommendation
- **Verdict: the year-round flag does NOT survive as a stocking classification, and months [12,1,2] are wrong.** Reclassify to `seasonal-stocked` with months **[3,4,5,6] + [10]** (Mar–Jun biweekly put-and-take, ~8,500 rainbows/yr, plus the annual fall Delayed Harvest stocking in RMSP, week-of dates ranging Sep 28–Oct 27, 2019–2026). If the ledger's month list carries the DH event, use [3,4,5,6,10]; if DH is tracked separately, [3,4,5,6] with a DH note.
- Remove Dec–Feb: no scheduled winter stocking exists in any recovered document 2003–2026. The only winter stocking ever found is the one-time January 2019 DH creation event.
- Preserve in notes (do not discard): agency-documented wild reproduction (R4 2018: "finest populations of wild Rainbow and Brown trout"; standing "excellent wild trout populations" statement through 2023) and the RMSP C&R season Oct 1–Feb 28 — trout ARE present and catchable-release through winter on fall-stocked + wild fish, which likely generated the ledger's winter months. If the ledger's year-round category admits documented wild/holdover fisheries (as its standard reads), Doe River could alternatively remain year-round on that clause — but then months must be replaced by [3,4,5,6,10] and the Dec–Feb stocking implication dropped.
- Gaps: 2016–2017 and 2021–2023 full-season grids unrecovered; DH fall stocking is a single row/event in schedules (its exact completion dates appear only in the completed feeds, which roll off quickly).
