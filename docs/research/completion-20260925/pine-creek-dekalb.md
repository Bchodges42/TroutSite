# PINE CREEK (DeKalb County) — evidence log
Retrieval date for all sources: 2026-09-25. Research only; no agency/business contact.

## 1. IDENTITY

**Verdict (high confidence):** TWRA's "Pine Creek" (Region III, DeKalb) is the ~14.5-mile stream (27 sq mi drainage) flowing north from the Smithville area (DeKalb Co) into the **Caney Fork embayment of Center Hill Lake at Caney Fork River Mile 68.8**. USGS stations: Pine Creek Springs Near Smithville (03422619) and Pine Creek Tributary Near Smithville (03422680). Local landmark "Pine Creek Falls" sits on the stocked reach. TDEC's Caney Fork watershed plan describes it as a "small, scenic fishing stream." Stocked reach is private land with two signed bridge crossings.

Disambiguation (NOT this water): "Pine Creek, Marion Co" (Region 3 row in schedules 2003–2017 — a separate stream, stocked Mar/Apr/May in those years); "Piney Creek" Rhea Co (11/18-type fall/winter rows); "Sink Creek" (paired with Pine Creek on the TWRA sheet but a different water); "Big Rock Creek" R2 (a put-and-take pond/creek near Carthage — different).

Sources (identity):

## 4. SPECIES
Rainbow trout only (GIS 900+900 rainbow; all schedules; 2026 JSON "Rainbow Trout").

## 5. TYPE + CONFIDENCE
- Type: **seasonal late-winter/spring stocked stream** — February, March, April (3 events) in the modern era (2018–2026); earlier era March–May with many years March–April only. No summer/fall/winter-season events in any year.
- Confidence: months = HIGH (24 consecutive primary schedules + 2024 completion); identity = HIGH; pre-2010 week-level precision = MEDIUM (glyph mapping).

## 6. ESTABLISHES VS NOT
- Establishes: identity (Caney Fork/Center Hill embayment stream at Smithville, DeKalb Co); two private-land bridge access sites (Big Rock Rd S1 35.90799/-85.77146; Student Home Rd S2 35.90481/-85.78396; ~900 rainbow each); continuous 2003–2026 spring schedule; shift to a February start from 2018.
- Does NOT establish: fish counts per event by year; any fall/winter stocking; brown/brook presence.

## 7. CONTRADICTIONS / CAVEATS
- "Pine Creek" rows in 2003–2017 schedules also include a Marion County Pine Creek — county column disambiguates (DeKalb row continuous). No source conflict for the DeKalb water itself.
- 2006–2015 third-week placement varies between a second April week and early May in different PDF captures; recorded as Mar–Apr (occasionally May) with week-level MEDIUM confidence.
- Web corroboration is thin (small private-land water; no dedicated angler pages found); verdict rests on TWRA primary documents, which is appropriate.

## 9. RECOMMENDATION
Classify **Pine Creek (DeKalb)** as a **seasonal stocked stream: February, March, and April** (three weekly-window events; current-era pattern 2018–2026), preceded by a March–(April)May pattern from 2003–2017 — in all cases a spring-only, no-summer/fall program. Identity notes: Caney Fork embayment (CFRM 68.8) of Center Hill Lake near Smithville; stocked at two private-land bridge crossings (Big Rock Rd; Student Home Rd; ~900 rainbow each); distinct from the Marion County Pine Creek that shares the destination name in older schedules.
