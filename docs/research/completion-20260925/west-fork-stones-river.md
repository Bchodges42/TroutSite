## 5. Contradictions / flags
1. MTFF "December to June" (©2019 club page) vs agency record: no June events ever; last post-February event = 5/18/2018 (completed); last March events = through 3/6/2020 (Nice Mill). Manson Pike has NEVER had a March row in any recovered season (last events always Feb or earlier). MTFF claim = LOW/legacy.
2. March leg: supported for NICE MILL by five seasons of planned March stockings (2013, 2014, 2017, 2018, 2019, 2020 seasons) but by NO March event since 2020, and not at all for Manson Pike. Ledger-wide month 3 is therefore partial-water and stale.
3. 2021-22 TWRA coldwater table lists Manson Pike (2/2/2022) but OMITS Nice Mill — single-season omission anomaly.
4. Warmwater leg is community-science + museum based: NO TWRA electrofishing/creel report or TDEC fish survey specific to the WF was located (TDEC biological data has zero WF stations). Water-specificity is nonetheless satisfied at the named water (2 iNat smallmouth AT the stocking sites/reach, 1970 museum smallmouth at the WF/Middle Fork junction, Lepomis records, NPS 303d note).
5. 303(d) listing is for the warmwater stream as such — context only, not a trout statement.
