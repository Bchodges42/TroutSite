## Identity

- TVA mainstem impoundment of the Tennessee River at TN mile 530 (dam completed 1942), Watts Bar Dam at 35.6218,-84.7801 (OSM Nominatim, retrieved 2026-09-25). 39,600 acres, 722 miles of shoreline; Rhea/Meigs counties (+ Roane via the Clinch arm); near Spring City, Rockwood, Harriman, Kingston, Lenoir City. TWRA Region 3.
- Notable tributaries per TWRA: "the Clinch River, Emory River, White's Creek, and Piney Creek" — i.e., the Melton Hill/Clinch and Emory confluences sit inside this reservoir's arms.

## Recommendation
CONFIRM `warmwater-focus` for Watts Bar Lake, confidence high. The agency profile documents an intensively managed warmwater program — FLMB since 2015, striped bass since 1964, walleye since 2011, crappie, sauger, high-regard smallmouth (with an Alabama-bass invasion warning), plus a resident muskellunge regulation — and the CURRENT TWRA creel report (Sept 12–14, 2026) plus a Sept 21, 2026 BFL tournament document the live fishery. Zero trout anywhere in the agency description; 2026 trout schedule/ArcGIS have no Watts Bar/Emory rows; GBIF shows only scattered washdown-type rainbow presence (Dec 2024 main-channel iNat record). Presence ≠ fishery — no trout classification change warranted.
