## 1. Identity and coordinates

- Wikipedia, "Little Buffalo River (Tennessee)" (retrieved 2026-09-25): 18.1-mile tributary of the Buffalo River; "rises a short distance south of Deerfield in northern Lawrence County, Tennessee, near Laurel Hill" (980 ft); "empties into the Buffalo River near State Route 99 in Lewis County" (673 ft; mouth 35.4504, -87.5314); flows through Lawrence then Lewis County; HUC-12 060400040106 (Duck basin HUC 06040004); NO dam, impoundment, or lake mentioned. Major tributary: Jacks Branch.
- GNIS / The National Map gazetteer, Feature ID 1291406 (searched 2026-09-25): Class Stream; primary county Lewis County, TN.
- USGS Water Data monitoring location USGS-03603610 "LITTLE BUFFALO RIVER NEAR DEERFIELD, TN" (fetched 2026-09-25): Lawrence County; site type Stream; hydrologic unit "Buffalo" 06040004; drainage area 6.1 sq mi at that point; field measurements 1961-09-29 to 1972-08-31.
- "Dammed stream forming Little Buffalo Lake" hypothesis: FALSE. No impoundment in Wikipedia/GNIS/USGS; all TWRA sites are classed "stream".
- TDEC/EPA surface-water use classifications (Tennessee rule 0400-40-04; surfaced via search 2026-09-25) list "Little Buffalo River, Mile 0.0 to Origin".

## 5. Contradictions

- Incoming months [12,1,2]: refuted (section 2-3). The May 2022/May 2024 completions that puzzled the prior pass are exactly on-pattern (May is a core month).
- A web-search summary (2026-09-25) asserted a TWRA Facebook post put ~250 rainbow trout into Little Buffalo under the "2025-26 winter program". No primary confirmation exists: the 2026 JSON has no Winter row for the water, the archived winter lists omit it, and news coverage naming it in the winter program did not surface (searched; Facebook source itself not accessible). Flagged as unverified secondary noise; primary data keep the water out of Dec-Feb.
- Search engines conflate the Arkansas Little Buffalo River (Jasper/Newton Co.) with this stream; Arkansas hits were discarded.
- 2010 row was mis-transcribed "Montgomery Little Buffalo River" and 2011-2013 rows appeared as "DeKalb/Macon/Cumberland Little Buffalo River" in prior pdftotext extraction — an artifact of the rotated (90°) PDF layout; the PDFs themselves show "Lawrence Little Buffalo River" every year.
