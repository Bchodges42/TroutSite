## Water identification

- Stream: Greasy Creek, Polk County (City field "Benton"), Cherokee NF Ocoee Ranger District, along SR-30 (Greasy Creek / Hwy 30 corridor); Ocoee drainage headwaters area. Dispersed camping incl. informal "Camp McRoy" on the creek.
- TWRA program: Region 3 "Spring" seasonal small-stream rainbow program. Part of the Polk four-creek group (Big Lost Creek, Goforth, Greasy, Spring) whose stocking-era special regulations (Friday closures) were retired to statewide rules in the 2018 cycle.
- Distinct from: other Greasy Creeks in TN (e.g., Greasy Creek in Hancock Co.); the stocked one here is Polk/Region 3 (all four ArcGIS sites Polk).
