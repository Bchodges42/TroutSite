## What the organization is — trust calibration

**A nonprofit fly-fishing club, not a charter/guide company.** Quotes from
about.html: "Join us the first Wednesday of each month at the Ellington
Agricultural Center in Nashville. Meetings begin in the T.W.R.A. Region II
office at 7:00 pm"; membership dues payable to the treasurer; activities are
"Fly tying, fly casting, rod building and entomology" + a monthly newsletter.
The only affiliation named: "MTFF is an Affiliate Member of Fly Fishing
International." **No corporate sponsors appear on the about page** (a Sponsors
link exists; contents not inspected). They meet in TWRA's Region II office
space — adjacency, not endorsement.

**Evidence class:** member-written river guides = dated firsthand-report-class
SECONDARY source. Excellent for leads, reach/access knowledge, and
corroborating agency facts on waters their members actually fish. Not
agency-grade: the Elk page's trout claims ("stocked March thru December",
"holdovers", "no stream-bred trout") carry no citations. Per the project's
rules (and the owner's single-catch threshold), club narrative corroborates —
it never establishes — a water-level classification.
