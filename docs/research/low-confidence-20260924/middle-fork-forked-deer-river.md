## QUESTION 1 — TROUT STOCKING/OCCURRENCE EVER: NO EVIDENCE

A1. TWRA live 2026 Trout Stocking Schedule JSON (616 rows, retrieved 2026-09-24): ZERO rows on any fork of the Forked Deer; no Crockett County rows at all; Madison County's ONLY trout water is LAKE GRAHAM (1/8/2026 + TBD 12/2026, Winter, Rainbow Trout) — a 575-acre TWRA lake ~10 mi E of Jackson, NOT the fork and NOT a Jackson city pond. Any "Jackson TN trout stocking" hit resolves to Lake Graham and must be attributed to the lake. (Gibson's only trout water = Milan City Pond, also off the fork.) Confidence HIGH.
A2. TWRA ArcGIS TWRA_Trout_Stocking_Locations layer, statewide query UPPER(Site_Name)/UPPER(StreamName) LIKE '%FORKED%' (retrieved 2026-09-24): EMPTY — no trout stocking site named Forked anywhere in Tennessee; hence none on the fork. Confidence HIGH.
A3. GBIF Salmonidae (taxon_key 8615) in basin polygon 35.50–36.10 N / −89.80–−88.40 W (api.gbif.org, retrieved 2026-09-24): count 0 — the polygon fully contains the fork. Coverage control: Micropterus salmoides = 7 in-polygon (incl. Madison Co 1973 University of Alabama ichthyology voucher; Gibson 2006/2012). Confidence MEDIUM-HIGH (non-detection ≠ absence).
A4. iNaturalist TN Salmonidae (place 45, taxon 47520) in the same bbox (retrieved 2026-09-24): 0.
A5. USGS NAS api/v2 (raw JSON, retrieved 2026-09-24): Crockett County = 0 records; Madison County = 4 records, NONE salmonid and NONE on the Middle Fork (American shad 1876 "Forked Deer River at Jackson" [South Fork HUC], common carp "North Fork Forked Deer (HUC-8)" 1993, freshwater jellyfish Lake Graham 1999, cane toad 2020). CAUTION/discard: an earlier NAS-facing fetch summary produced a "Middle Forked Deer River area, 1999, grass carp stocked" row attributed to Lauderdale County — the raw JSON contains NO such record (grep for 'iddle' across all four county files: no Middle Fork row exists in any). Treat that row as a summarizer artifact; do not cite it as fork fish documentation.
A6. TDEC Final 2012 303(d) List (archived PDF text-extracted 2026-09-24, print p. 143): Middle Fork rows — "TN08010204 | 010-1000 | MIDDLE FORK FORKED DEER RIVER | Crockett, Gibson | 9.5 [mi] | Escherichia coli | Collection System Failure | Category 4a. Warmwater impairment causes only; no trout designation or mention anywhere in the Forked Deer pages. Confidence HIGH.
A7. TWRA regs (fishing-regulation-exceptions.html, retrieved 2026-09-24): the fork is covered only by the West TN rivers crappie rule — "Forked Deer, Hatchie, Loosahatchie, Obion, and Wolf Rivers (includes tributaries): Crappie: 30 per day, no length limit." No trout regulation. (The rule's explicit "includes tributaries" is what carries the Middle Fork.)

Bottom line Q1: no trout occurrence, stocking site, schedule row, regulation, or crowd/museum observation exists for the Middle Fork in any dataset checked. Crockett County has zero NAS records of any kind; Madison's only trout water is a lake.
