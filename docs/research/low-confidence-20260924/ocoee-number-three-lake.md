# Ocoee Number Three Lake (Polk County, TN) — Trout-Occurrence Evidence Research Log

Water: Ocoee Number Three Lake = TVA "Ocoee No. 3 Reservoir" (alt. "Ocoee Lake No. 3"), impoundment of the Ocoee River behind TVA Ocoee Dam No. 3 (dam at 35.04056, -84.46667; dam built 1941–43, opened 1943-04-30). Lake polygon (OpenStreetMap relation, retrieved 2026-09-24): center 35.02739, -84.42890; bbox 35.0124–35.0424 N, -84.4699 to -84.4093 W — a narrow gorge impoundment running ~SE from the dam upriver toward the TN/GA line. Acreage DISPUTED across sources: 360 ac (Wikipedia, TVA-cited) vs 544 ac (Lake-Link) vs 553 ac (Fishroulette) vs ~600 ac (onWater) vs catalog's ~550 ac. Upstream of Parksville Lake (Ocoee No. 1 impoundment), which is a DIFFERENT water.
Ledger state entering: unresolved with IDENTITY ISSUE (claimed TWRA alias "Hiwassee Lake (443 ac)" unverified).
Research date: 2026-09-24 (all retrievals 2026-09-24).

## IDENTITY / REACH DISCIPLINE (resolved with caveats)

- OSM Nominatim (nominatim.openstreetmap.org, q="Ocoee Number Three Lake", polygon_geojson, retrieved 2026-09-24): relation 13269305-class water=reservoir named "Ocoee Number Three Lake, Polk County, East Tennessee, Tennessee" (polygon, 66k coordinate chars, bbox above). A second, tiny separate way polygon (35.0751, -84.4831, ~15 m) carries the same name in OSM — mapping noise, not the pool. Establishes: the colloquial/OSM name matches the TVA impoundment in the Ocoee gorge; NOT Parksville Lake, NOT the Hiwassee. Confidence HIGH for geometry.
- Wikipedia "Ocoee Dam No. 3" (en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ocoee_Dam_No._3, rev 1338327726, retrieved 2026-09-24): dam "impounds the 360-acre (150 ha) Ocoee No. 3 Reservoir, which stretches 11 miles (18 km) upriver to the Tennessee-Georgia state line"; "Ocoee Reservoir No. 3 has 360 acres (150 ha) of water surface and 24 miles (39 km) of shoreline"; powerhouse sits several miles downstream fed by a 2.5-mi conduit; "Major recreational releases are typically scheduled for weekends during the summer months." Dam coordinates 35°02′26″N 84°28′0″W. Confidence MEDIUM-HIGH (tertiary, TVA-cited; TVA.com itself 403-blocked to all fetchers this pass).
- TWRA naming (ArcGIS layers, below): TWRA's only "Ocoee" wording anywhere in its trout layers is "Parksville Reservoir (Ocoee Lake #1)" — i.e., TWRA reserves "Ocoee Lake #1" for PARKSVILLE, confirming the dam-number convention. No TWRA dataset contains "Ocoee No. 3," "Ocoee #3," "Ocoee Lake No. 3," or "Hiwassee Lake" as a water name. Confidence HIGH.
- ALIAS REFUTED: "Hiwassee Lake (443 ac)" — TWRA_Trout_Stocking_Locations statewide query StreamName LIKE '%Hiwassee%' returns 9 features, ALL StreamName = "Hiwassee River" (the Apalachia-dam tailwater in Reliance/Delano, Polk Co). No "Hiwassee Lake" exists in any TWRA trout dataset. The only real "Hiwassee Lake/Reservoir" of angling fame is the NC impoundment (thousands of acres, Cherokee Co NC) — not 443 ac, not this pool. Catalog alias should be treated as UNSOURCED and removed. Confidence HIGH.
- Acreage contradiction: 360 ac (Wikipedia/TVA-cited, 24 mi shoreline) vs 544 ac (Lake-Link #135205) vs 553 ac (fishroulette) vs ~600 ac (onWater). Catalog "~550 ac" matches aggregators, not the TVA-cited figure. TVA primary figure still unverified (tva.com blocked); flag acreage as contested, prefer 360 ac pending TVA primary. Confidence LOW on the number, HIGH on the conflict.

## BOTTOM LINE / RECOMMENDATION
**Recommendation: WARMWATER-FOCUS** (classify the pool by its demonstrated warmwater fishery), with two attached caveats: (1) **species list itself UNRESOLVED** — no agency survey of any kind was located, so the warmwater call rests on community species data (Fishbrain top species yellow perch / spotted bass / largemouth; iNat warmwater-only), not on agency documentation; (2) **acreage contested** (TVA-cited 360 ac vs aggregator ~544–553 ac vs catalog ~550 ac — verify against TVA primary before publishing).
Reasoning: four independent TWRA datasets spanning 2016→2026 (2026 schedule JSON; current stocking-locations layer statewide; StockedTrout2016 completed-releases layer; archived 2024 destination list) contain ZERO records for this pool under ANY name — while all neighboring waters (Parksville Reservoir, its tailwater, Goforth/Tumbling/Greasy/Spring creeks, McKamy Lake) are richly documented, so TWRA coverage of this exact geography is demonstrably good and the negative is meaningful, not an artifact. The catalog's TWRA alias "Hiwassee Lake (443 ac)" is refuted (no such TWRA water; only "Hiwassee River" exists). Environmental context (AMD-legacy sediments, JEQ 2008; 2009-01-04 sludge release fish kill) argues against any coldwater-management history. No holdover/trout occurrence from the pool itself exists anywhere in GBIF/iNat/community data; the single salmonid record in the surrounding bbox is a 2005 museum specimen from stocked Tumbling Creek. If the catalog keeps an entry at all: name = Ocoee No. 3 Reservoir (TVA) / Ocoee Number Three Lake (local); class = warmwater impoundment, unmanaged/undocumented fishery; remove the alias and the trout implications; drop "~550 ac" to "360 ac (TVA-cited; aggregators say 544–553 — unverified)".
