# Yellow Creek (Houston/Dickson Counties, TN) — Trout-Occurrence Evidence Research Log

Water: Yellow Creek — lower Cumberland River tributary, Middle TN. Rises in SE Dickson County (Pond/Hortense/Ruskin communities), flows NW through Dickson and Houston counties, enters the Cumberland River at the Lake Barkley impoundment embayment at the Hwy 149 crossing (~36.414 N, -87.580 W, at the Houston/Montgomery county line near Palmyra). ~21–25 mi long; drainage 103 sq mi at the Ellis Mills gauge (RM 13.1). Ledger state: unresolved — no trout survey/stocking/dated record; community reports describe warmwater bass/panfish.
Research date: 2026-09-24 (all retrieval dates = 2026-09-24).
Researcher constraint: research only; no agency/business/author/angler contact; no git writes; no writes except this file.

## RECOMMENDATION

**WARMWATER-FOCUS — upgrade from "unresolved."**
Reasoning: (1) The agency negatives are as broad as they get without an internal TWRA query: zero Yellow-named rows in the 616-row 2026 stocking schedule; zero Yellow-named sites in the 730-site current master layer AND the 796-site 2016 baseline layer; no mention in the (validated-extraction) statewide trout plan; not one of the 13 tailwater trout waters; while the SAME county (Houston) carries two actively stocked trout streams and Dickson carries two stocked park impoundments — the schedule demonstrably covers this area and passes Yellow Creek by. (2) Independent occurrence data agrees: GBIF Salmonidae = 0 in the watershed polygon vs 5,492 largemouth-bass records in the identical polygon (in-polygon samples verified). iNat adds little (fish under-sampled) and NAS was blocked — noted as caveats, not hidden. (3) Dated warmwater infrastructure exists on the water: a USACE/TWRA concrete boat ramp on the Lake Barkley embayment at the mouth (field-verified Nov 2025), a ~1,810-ac TWRA Barkley WMA tract there, a 46-year continuous USGS gauge (1981-2026), and TDEC ambient sampling (2001, 2005-06). The mouth reach is Barkley impoundment water, warmwater by definition; no trout tailwater exists on this Cumberland reach. (4) No LEAD anywhere suggests trout: no historical plant, no anomaly record, no single catch. Watershed-lake transfer rules do not apply (there is no lake program here at all).
Key gaps: no method-documented fish species list for the creek itself (TDEC/NRSA sampling was chemistry-only; Etnier & Starnes unchecked); the ledger's community bass/panfish reports remain uncorroborated by a dated public source this pass (search-engine outage); USGS NAS county records unverified (blocked). Suggest labeling: "warmwater (Cumberland/Lake Barkley tributary; agency-documented flow/access; no trout evidence in any category; mouth embayment straddles onto Montgomery Co at Palmyra)."
