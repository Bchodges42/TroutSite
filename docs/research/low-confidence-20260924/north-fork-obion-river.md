# North Fork Obion River (Northwest Tennessee) — Trout-Occurrence Evidence Research Log

Water: North Fork Obion River, northwest TN — one of four principal forks (North, Middle, South, Rutherford) of the Obion River system, the primary surface drainage of northwest TN; rises in the Holly Springs Formation uplands of Henry/Weakley Co, flows WNW through Weakley Co (Latham, McConnell, Martin) past Union City to the Rives area, Obion Co, where it joins the Obion main stem. Ledger state: limited / warmwater-focus (TWRA West TN crappie rule 30/day). Catalog calls it part of a "first-class regional warmwater fishing corridor."
Research date: 2026-09-24 (all retrieval dates = 2026-09-24).
Researcher constraint: research only; no agency/business/author/angler contact; no git writes; no writes except this log file.

## QUESTION 4 — REACH DEFINITION (no backwater; channelized free-flowing)

- Headwaters: Holly Springs uplands on the Henry–Weakley line area; highest TDEC/USGS fixes = NFOBI040.6HN at HWY 69 Henry Co (RM 40.6) and USGS 07025300/07025310 at Jones Mill (36.446/−88.466). Dickinson: forks each ≈50 river miles, system "originates in the uplands of Henry, Weakley, and Carroll Counties."
- Course: WNW through Weakley Co (Latham RM ≈ between 26.5–40.6; McConnell; Martin, RM ≈ 18 by the NFOBI018.0WY Hwy 45E label) then past SR-22/Union City (RM ≈ 10.7, NFOBI010.7OB HWY 22) to Mt Pelia Rd near Rives (RM 5.9, NFOBI005.9OB).
- Mouth/confluence: joins the Obion main stem in the Rives area (NW of the town of Rives), Obion Co — between USGS 07025600 (NF near Rives, 36.3515/−89.0312) and 07025900 (main stem nr Rives, 36.2767/−89.0601). The main-stem name begins further upstream at the Kenton reach (07024800, 36.2459/−88.9745), where Dickinson's "convergence of these forks near the Weakley-Obion County line forms the main river" applies to the Middle+South Fork merge. Residual ambiguity (exact NF–main-stem junction pin) noted; GNIS/WBD not pulled this pass.
- Backwater: NONE. The Obion system lies above any reservoir backwater; the forks are channelized free-flowing lowland streams (channelization "begun in the lower river... proceeded upstream to the point where the major tributaries come together and then for several miles up the tributaries," halted by Court Order 1970 — Dickinson; Obion-Forked Deer Basin Authority + USACE per Wikipedia). "Old river slough of North Fork Obion" appears as a 1973 collecting-habitat label (Dickinson appendix) — the only slough/backwater feature named on the NF.
- Downstream continuity: NF water reaches the Mississippi via the Obion–Forked Deer merge ≈3 mi from the river's mouth (Dickinson).

## RECOMMENDATION

**WARMWATER-FOCUS — keep, with confidence raised from "limited."**
Reasoning: (1) Trout negatives are unusually broad and multi-source: zero rows on the North Fork in the 616-row 2026 TWRA schedule; zero trout stocking sites anywhere on the system except three urban park ponds (Martin City Pond and Union City Reelfoot Packing Site are IN this drainage but are impoundments, not the river); zero Salmonidae in GBIF (0, controls 12/2/38) and iNat (0, control 11) across the watershed polygon; zero Salmonidae in USGS NAS across all six watershed counties (an API that does track trout introductions and worked this pass); and a 1973 91-page systematic survey of the whole Obion system (~90 species, 183 collections, museum-vouchered, 53 collections on the NF alone) with zero trout. No historical planting paper trail located. (2) Warmwater documentation is dated and official-plus-press: the fork sits inside TWRA's named West TN crappie exception ("Obion... Rivers (including their tributaries): Crappie: 30 per day"); a 2012 dated press float-fishing account covers the Rives reach by name; TDEC biorecon/SQBANK benthic monitoring 2001–2020 at 6–7 named stations; a 1961 Game and Fish survey of the system is referenced in the literature. (3) Reach definition is pinned: Henry/Weakley headwaters (Jones Mill/Hwy 69) → Martin → Union City → Rives area mouth at the main stem; channelized, free-flowing, no reservoir backwater. Not "unresolved": every stocked/observed trout lane returns zero with controls. Not "seasonal-stocked": the only seasonal trout nearby is Martin City Pond (city impoundment at Martin — label separately if the map wants it).
Key gaps: no published fish SPECIES LIST with method for the post-1973 NF itself (TDEC biological data on the fork is benthic-only); no TWRA-developed access (zero ramps) so no TWRA creel/survey infrastructure; tournament/guide lane and TDEC tissue/advisory lane both exhausted early by search rate limits — neither is likely to produce trout-relevant evidence. Suggest labeling: "warmwater (documented bass/crappie/catfish-class fishery; 1973 systematic survey; no trout evidence, any category)."
