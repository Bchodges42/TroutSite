## IDENTITY / REACH DISCIPLINE — THREE DIFFERENT WATERS (pinned by coordinates)

1. **Parksville Reservoir (the lake)** = "Ocoee Lake #1" in TWRA's own naming (TWRA_Trout_Stocking_Locations OBJECTID 581 Site_Name verbatim "Parksville Reservoir (Ocoee Lake #1)", point 35.09887, -84.63244 near the dam end; OBJECTID 582 mid-lake 35.11248, -84.58832). ~1,930 ac (tn.gov). Any catalog hit for "Ocoee Lake #1/Ocoee No. 1 (lake)" is THIS water, not the tailwater.
2. **The tailwater reach (this water)** = Ocoee River below the dam to the Hiwassee. TWRA stocking site: OBJECTID 755 "Parksville Dam" / StreamName "Parksville Lake Tailwater", 35.09573, -84.65144 (Program "Tailwater", Species rainbow, NumStocked 1,650, Management TVA, sunrise-sunset hours, paved lot >25 spaces). Tailwater_Trout FeatureServer polyline "Ocoee River": Dam "Parksville Dam", first coordinate [-84.65673, 35.09831], Shape__Length ≈18,121 ft (~3.4 mi, 4 paths). TDEC WQP stations on the reach: TDECWPC-OCOEE011.9PO "OCOEE RIVER BELOW PARKSVILLE DAM" (35.0975, -84.6555 area), OCOEE002.6PO (35.18579), OCOEE001.0PO "NEAR MOUTH BOAT LAUNCH AT TWO RIVERS CAMPGROUND" (35.1981). USGS gage 03564500 "OCOEE RIVER AT PARKSVILLE, TN" (35.09689, -84.65437) sits ~250 m below the dam.
3. **The upper Ocoee river** (whitewater gorge, Ocoee Dams No. 2 and No. 3, Ocoee Whitewater Center) = a DIFFERENT water with NO TWRA trout stocking points anywhere (statewide StreamName LIKE '%Ocoee%' in the current stocking layer = 0 features); trout claims for the upper gorge are at best washdown/residual fish — unverified.

Cherokee NF question: the reach below the dam is attributed by TWRA to TVA dam-reserve management (Management "TVA" on the stocking site), not USFS; Cherokee NF lands adjoin the reservoir and the gorge ABOVE the dam (Goforth Creek etc. carry Management "USFS"). The catalog premise "the Ocoee No. 1 reach passes through Cherokee NF" is NOT supported by TWRA data for the tailwater reach itself — MEDIUM confidence (no land-ownership GIS pulled this pass).

## BOTTOM LINE / RECOMMENDATION
**Recommendation: SEASONAL-STOCKED** (rainbow trout, March–May put-and-take tailwater; no documented post-May fishery).
Reasoning: three independent TWRA sources agree on a single-species rainbow program confined to March–May (2026 schedule row; Tailwater_Trout season text "March through May"; the TVA-managed 1,650-per-event dam site in both the current and 2016 layers). The holdover open question resolves NEGATIVE-by-absence plus mechanism: no dated post-May catch, survey, or holdover documentation exists anywhere retrieved; the only temperature record (TDEC grab samples 2001–2008) shows summer maxima 23.8–25.3 °C on the reach — above sustained rainbow preference and consistent with guide language that trout angling is "excellent especially in cooler months" with only dawn/dusk summer fishing; no continuous thermal record exists at the dam (USGS gage discharge-only; TVA data blocked). The reach is short (~3.4 mi mapped), flow-pulsed, and receives washdown of its trout into the Hiwassee's own fishery. Residual uncertainties to note in the ledger: (1) thermal evidence is 18–25 years old and grab-sampled; (2) agency survey of the reach: none found; (3) identity discipline — keep three waters separate (reservoir / tailwater / upper river), and note the Cherokee-NF premise for the tailwater is unsupported (TWRA attributes the site to TVA management).
