Water: Bradley Creek, Coffee County TN (upper/main stem: Hillsboro–Prairie Plains–AEDC; drains through Woods Reservoir into the Elk River system). Same-name exposure: Bradley Creek/Rutherford-Co. Milton area (HUC 05130203), Bradley Creek/Hawkins Co. (06010104), Bradley County TN — all excluded by HUC/coordinate checks below.
Research date: 2026-09-24 (all retrievals 2026-09-24).
Ledger at start: unresolved — "COOL GAUGED temperature record" (interesting wrinkle), no trout program/survey/dated observation, shared Fishbrain page flag.
Researcher constraint: research only; no agency/business contact; no writes except this file.

## HEADLINE FINDING

The cool-water wrinkle is REAL and now quantified: the USGS gauge on upper Bradley Creek (03578500, nr Prairie Plains) has 33 discrete water-temperature measurements 1988–2020; the 10 June–August samples never exceeded 19.0 °C (max 19.0 on 2004-08-02; typical summer grab results 15–19 °C). TDEC's ambient stations on the same creek agree (summer maxima 17.0–18.6 °C). This is a genuinely cold-for-Middle-TN, spring-fed karst stream (the USGS station list itself is largely a network of named springs: Finney Spring, Long Spring, Pond Spring/Fults Spring, "Unnamed Spring at Bradley Cr" x3).

BUT every evidence lane for actual TROUT is negative:
- No stocking row, any year checked (2003, 2018, 2019, 2020, 2022, 2026), and ZERO Bradley sites in TWRA's ~730-site ArcGIS trout layer (statewide LIKE sweep).
- Coffee County has no trout stocking destination at all in the current schedule (nearest Region-2 coldwater sites are Cowan City Park/Boiling Fork Creek, Normandy TW, Tims Ford TW — all different waters).
- Museum collections (UA Ichthyology, 1968 + 1984, at Hwy 41 Hillsboro and Pond Spring) and iNaturalist (34 research-grade fish obs in the drainage) document a cool-water but NON-trout assemblage: banded sculpin, spring cavefish, flame chub, bluemask darter, studfish, sunfish. Zero trout specimens/observations.
- Community layer (Fishbrain page for Bradley Creek): top species largemouth bass, bluegill. No trout catch reports found anywhere.
- Context waterbody Woods Reservoir (AEDC) is TWRA-managed warmwater (top-3 crappie lake; walleye, largemouth, channel cat); no trout program; PCB advisory.

The single Oncorhynchus mykiss iNat record inside the wide bounding box is at Estill Springs (Elk River below Woods/Tims Ford — tailwater trout context), not Bradley Creek; casual grade, no date. LEAD only, not assignable to Bradley Creek.

Bottom line: capacity for cold water is documented; trout presence/program is not. The thermal record alone cannot make it a trout water. Recommendation: unresolved, leaning warmwater-focus (see bottom).

## RECOMMENDATION

**Unresolved, leaning warmwater-focus.**
Reasoning:
- The ledger's interesting wrinkle is confirmed and refined: upper Bradley Creek is a spring-fed, genuinely cool stream (summer grab maxima 15–19 °C across two agencies, 1988–2020) — thermally CAPABLE of holding trout year-round, a rare condition in the Middle TN whisky-karst Barrens.
- But trout evidence is zero in every evidentiary type: never a schedule row (2003–2026 checked), never a site in TWRA's master trout layer, no trout specimen in 1968/1984 museum collections on the creek, no trout photo in iNat, no trout catch on the community page, no trout program at receiving-water Woods Reservoir. Under the stated standards, capacity ≠ presence; the documented assemblage (sculpin/spring-cavefish/flame-chub guild plus reservoir warmwater) is the actual fishery.
- A seasonal-stocked classification has no support either (no planting record exists, and Coffee County has no stocking destination at all). "Trout" classification is unsupported.
- Path to reclassification: (a) obtain TWRA Region 2 coldwater records for Coffee County (none located in public documents); (b) any dated trout catch photo ON Bradley Creek would flip it to "lead"; (c) a continuous summer temperature logger would firm up (not change) the capacity case.
- Practical note for the catalog: the stream's cool thermal signature is real and worth a remark, but the fishery and all observed fish are warmwater/coolwater non-trout; the nearest actual trout fishery is the Tims Ford tailwater Elk River reach and (marginally) Estill Springs, not Bradley Creek.
