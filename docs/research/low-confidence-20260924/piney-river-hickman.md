# Piney River (Hickman County, TN) — Trout-Occurrence Evidence Research Log

Water: Piney River (Middle Tennessee) — 23.7-mi Duck River tributary, Dickson→Hickman counties. Heads at the West Piney–East Piney confluence near Mount Sinai (Dickson Co); flows S-SE into Hickman Co; mouth (RM 0) at the Duck River confluence, "a few miles west-northwest of Centerville." Known paddling/fishing corridor (Dodd Hollow TWRA access, Pinewood Canoe & Camp outfitter). Ledger state: unresolved with SPECIFIC CONFLICT — a forum-level claim that the Piney system is trout-stocked vs. TWRA documents carrying trout rows only for the Rhea County Piney (a different river).
Research date: 2026-09-24 (all retrieval dates = 2026-09-24).
Researcher constraint: research only; no agency/business/author/angler contact; no git writes; only file written = this log.

## RECOMMENDATION

**WARMWATER-FOCUS for the Piney River (Hickman County) — resolve the ledger from "unresolved."**
Reasoning: (1) The stocking record is a complete, dated negative for THIS river: zero Piney rows for Hickman/Dickson in the 616-row 2026 schedule, the 730-site master layer, the 796-site 2016 layer, twelve archived schedules (2003–2015), and the 2018 page capture — while the same documents carry the trout program for the RHEA County Piney ("Piney Creek" 2003–2014, "Piney River" 2015→, Delayed Harvest, Piney Falls SNA sites) and, inside the Hickman Piney watershed, for the TRIBUTARY Mill Creek plus Cane Creek. Every "Piney" trout row in every TWRA document binds to Rhea (longitude -84.88, Spring City); every Hickman trout row binds to Cane/Mill Creek. The ledger's suspicion is confirmed. The Rhea Delayed Harvest association (foxchattanooga 2023; 2018 TWRA page; 2016 layer) attaches to the other river. (3) The river's own documentation is warmwater and dated: TWRA's own Dodd Hollow paddle-only access (2015), the Pinewood Canoe and Camp / Higher Pursuits Class I float guide with zero trout mention, Fishbrain smallmouth/catfish reports, 1966–2005 USGS Vernon monitoring, 2024 TDEC benthic/habitat sampling.
