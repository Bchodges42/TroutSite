## A. Decisive source — dated main-stem fish survey

### A1. USFS Final EIS: 1996 Olympic Whitewater Slalom Venue, Ocoee River, Polk County, Tennessee (Ocoee Ranger District, Cherokee NF; cooperating agency TVA). Management Bulletin R8-MB 68-A, March 1994.
- Publication date: March 1994. Field observation dates: Nov 6–7, 1992 (electrofishing); Jan 20–22, 1993 (sediment metals); cites TWRA 1988/1990 tributary work (Little et al. 1988, 1990) and TVA 1990–1991 reservoir work (Cox 1990; Clark 1991; Scott 1992; Myhr & Copeland 1990).
- Retrieved 2026-09-24 from Wikimedia Commons mirror of Internet Archive item CAT10698648.
- URL (direct PDF): https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/e/e7/Final_environmental_impact_statement_-_1996_Olympic_Whitewater_Slalom_Venue_Ocoee_River,_Polk_County,_Tennessee,_Ocoee_Ranger_District,_Cherokee_National_Forest_(IA_CAT10698648).pdf
- Commons file page: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Final_environmental_impact_statement_-_1996_Olympic_Whitewater_Slalom_Venue_Ocoee_River,_Polk_County,_Tennessee,_Ocoee_Ranger_District,_Cherokee_National_Forest_(IA_CAT10698648).pdf
- Relevant fields/tables: Section III.A.11 "Aquatic Resources" (pp. III-63 to III-69); Figure III.A.11-1 "Electrofishing Locations"; Table III.A.11-1 "Species List For Tributaries Of The Ocoee River"; Table III.A.11-2 (TWRA benthos, Rough/Rock creeks); Table III.A.10-4 (Jan 1993 sediment metals at 4 main-stem sites, ~RM 35, 26.6, 24.4, 18.5).
- Method/sites/effort:
  - MAIN CHANNEL: 7 sites on the Ocoee River main stem below Ocoee No. 3 Dam electrofished Nov 6–7, 1992 (Smith-Root Model 15A gas-powered backpack DC unit); representative habitats sampled (riffles/runs/shallow pools/backwaters <1.5 m); water 46.4–50 °F; flowing water present (recent rains — i.e., the channel was NOT dewatered during sampling).
  - DEWATERED-PRONE POOLS: two SCUBA divers searched ~150 m of deep pools between sites 5 and 6 (~45 min each; visibility excellent, max depth ~6 m) for benthic and midwater fishes — ZERO fish observed.
  - TRIBUTARIES (reach-adjacent, flagged — does NOT transfer to main stem): lower 100 m of Rough, Rock, Laurel, Williams creeks backpack-electrofished.
  - Benthos at main-stem sites 4, 6, 7 + 3 tributaries (D-frame kick net).
- MAIN-STEM RESULT (the reach's core negative evidence): "Only three fish at one site (stonerollers, site No. 4) were observed while electrofishing the Ocoee River below Ocoee No. 3 Dam… The fish community currently residing in the upper Ocoee River could be described as nearly non-existent." Divers found none. "The conclusion that the Ocoee River below No. 3 Dam does not support viable fish or macroinvertebrate communities agrees with the findings of Cox (1990)." The 3 stonerollers (Campostoma anomalum) were judged transients from Rough Creek. TROUT ON MAIN STEM: ZERO (omitted from an otherwise full method-documented effort).
- Sediment metals (main stem, Jan 1993): copper 2,360 mg/kg (Lake Ocoee) / 844 (No. 2 backwaters) / 210 (Blue Hole) / 148 (venue); zinc 2,760→292; lead 1,860→27.6 — gross bed contamination of the gorge consistent with Copper Basin mining; several parameters exceed tolerance/guideline values.
- TRIBUTARY species list (Table III.A.11-1; TRIBUTARY evidence — does NOT transfer):
  - Rough Creek (below No. 3 dam): rainbow trout (Oncorhynchus mykiss — adult AND juveniles collected Nov 1992; natural reproduction noted; "head waters had been stocked with this species, but not in recent years (Mayer, 1992)"), brook trout (Salvelinus fontinalis — historical record only, per Herrig 1993), stoneroller, blacknose dace, longnose dace, warpaint shiner (Luxilus coccogenis), whitetail shiner (Cyprinella galactura), creek chub, greenside darter — 9 species incl. historical; "relatively high biotic integrity."
  - Rock Creek (below No. 3 dam): TWRA electrofished 1990 — "no fish were observed" (pH 5.5, 19 cfs during attempt); "Only one fish species (rainbow trout) has been recorded from Rock Creek (Forest Service – CNF database)"; TWRA speculated summer drying.
  - Laurel Creek: creek chub only. Williams Creek: no fish.
- LAKE OCOEE / PARKSVILLE (different water; control): 1990 TWRA electrofishing — abundant largemouth bass (avg growth, consistent recruitment) + 12 other species; 20 species total observed in recent years; TVA 1991 Reservoir-IBI forebay rating "poor"; Fish Health Assessment "poor"; 1990 channel catfish with elevated Se and PCBs (>1.0 ug/g, no advisory then).
- Also documents: state designated uses for Ocoee + reservoirs RM 0–37.9 (fish & aquatic life etc.); Copper Basin erosion (Ocoee No. 3 Reservoir lost ~80% of volume).
- Source type: government survey/EIS (method-documented, multi-agency, dated). Confidence: HIGH.
- Establishes: (1) broad method-documented Nov-1992 survey of 7 main-stem sites + dived pools finding NO trout and essentially no fish at all — strong negative evidence for trout in the gorge main stem at that date; (2) trout (incl. wild-reproducing rainbows) present only in a TRIBUTARY (Rough Creek), plus a single historical Rock Creek rainbow — tributary-scoped, does not transfer.
- Does NOT establish: post-1992 main-stem status; recovery-era fish community (Cox 1990 lineage stops here in public web sources).

## J. Bottom line and recommendation

Evidence state for the UPPER Ocoee main stem:
- No stocking program has ever named this reach in agency registers found (2016 and current TWRA layers; 2026 schedule): the only Ocoee-named stockings are Parksville Reservoir and its tailwater — a different water. TWRA's in-reach programs are tributary-scoped (Goforth, Tumbling, Greasy; 2016 adds Turtletown).
- The best (and only dated, method-documented) main-stem fish survey found — 7-site electrofishing + dived pools, Nov 1992, USFS/TWA FEIS — recorded ZERO trout and essentially zero fish of any kind, and concluded the reach did not support viable fish or macroinvertebrate communities. Its trout records belong to Rough Creek (wild-reproducing rainbows; historical brook) and Rock Creek (one historical rainbow) — tributaries.
- The reach remained 303(d)-listed (2022) for copper/zinc/iron/sediment AND flow-regime modification (flume dewatering); WQP holds 1999–2009 metals series at the bypass reach but no biological assemblage data; GBIF/NAS/iNat hold no wild trout record with a main-stem locality.
- The flagged community lead (3 iNat rainbows, May 2025, at the whitewater course) resolves to a stocked-tributary coincidence: the points sit within ~15–30 m of TWRA's Goforth Creek Trail (S1) rainbow stocking site, weeks after spring stocking. Downgraded to LEAD.
- A genuine recovery-era fish community likely now exists in the reach (post-remediation; media reported fish returning by ~2016), but no public fish survey of the main stem after 1992 was found.

RECOMMENDATION: warmwater-focus (i.e., classify the upper Ocoee main stem as a non-trout water), with the caveat that "unresolved" would be defensible until a post-1992 survey surfaces.
