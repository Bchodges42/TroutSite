# South Fork Obion River (Northwest Tennessee) — Trout-Occurrence Evidence Research Log

Water: South Fork Obion River, northwest TN — one of four principal forks (North, Middle, South, Rutherford) of the Obion River system; rises in Carroll County (headwater tributaries also reach Henry Co), flows W through McKenzie (Carroll/Henry line), Christmasville, Skullbone, Greenfield, and Rutherford/Sidonia (Weakley Co), then NW to the Kenton area at the Weakley–Obion county line where the main Obion River forms; locally important general-fishing water. ## QUESTION 4 — REACH DEFINITION

- Headwaters: Carroll Co (USGS 07024350 nr McKenzie at 36.0745/−88.5634 is the uppermost named gauge; TDEC SFOBI029.0CR "BIG BUCK ROAD" at RM 29.0, Carroll); Dickinson: "South Fork Obion headwater tributaries in Carroll and Henry Counties."
- Course: W/NW through McKenzie (Carroll/Henry line) → Christmasville → Skullbone → Greenfield (RM ≈ 17–25 by the SFOBI series: 017.4WY Old SR-5, 025.0CR Hwy 79) → Rutherford → Sidonia (RM ≈ 9.7, Hwy 45E) → Obion Co.
- Mouth/confluence: the SF reaches the fork-convergence zone at the Weakley–Obion line: its last named crossings are Hwy 89 "Macedonia" (RM 6.0; tissue station 5614 and TWRA Hwy 89 ramp at 36.2465–67/−88.973–38), with the first main-stem "Obion River" gauge (07024800 NEAR KENTON) immediately W at 36.2459/−88.9745. Dickinson: "The convergence of these forks near the Weakley-Obion County line forms the main river." Residual ambiguity: the Middle Fork's Sidonia gauge (07024790, 36.2065/−88.9390) is co-located with the SF's (36.2053/−88.9398) — the two channelized courses run adjacent there; the exact Middle/South merge pin was not resolved this pass (GNIS/WBD not pulled). NAS/WBD treat the SF as discharging via huc10 "South Fork Obion River Outlet" (080102030607).
- Backwater: no reservoir backwater (system is above any impoundment); Dickinson documents a natural "Backwater of South Fork Obion River" collecting station and cypress minnow "backwater... near the South Fork Obion in Weakley County" (1971–73) — slackwater habitat exists locally; channelized reaches otherwise (Obion-Forked Deer Basin Authority + USACE works, halted by 1970 Court Order).
- The SF should NOT be drawn through the Rutherford Fork (parallel stream) or extended down the main stem past Kenton.
