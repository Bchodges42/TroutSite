# Beech River (Henderson/Decatur Counties, TN) — Trout-Occurrence Evidence Research Log

Water: Beech River — 38.3-mi regional Tennessee River tributary, West TN. Rises ~5 mi NW of Lexington (Henderson Co), flows through Lexington, then eastward into Decatur County (between Parsons and Decaturville), entering the Kentucky Lake (Kentucky Dam backwater) embayment near Perryville (mouth RM 0 at ~35.6165 N, -88.0376 W; Hwy 100 bridge at RM 0.4). Drainage 251 sq mi near the mouth. HUC-8 06040001 (Lower Tennessee-Beech). Ledger state: unresolved — no reach-specific fishery documentation; DISTINCT from Beech Lake, which carries the watershed's TWRA trout program.
Research date: 2026-09-24 (all retrieval dates = 2026-09-24).
Researcher constraint: research only; no agency/business/author/angler contact; no git writes; no writes except this file.

### The watershed-lake trout program (kept SEPARATE from the river)
A10. Beech Lake (a.k.a. Beech Reservoir) is the TVA-impounded Beech River itself at Lexington (Wikipedia; NWIS lake gauge 03594413 adjacent to the stocking site). Winter rainbow trout program: ~1,500/yr at a county-managed access (master layer NumStocked 1500, Management "County"); continuous 2021→2026 (guide 2024-adjacent cycle per A4; 2026 schedule A1). ANY trout hit attributed to "Beech" is a LAKE hit; per standing standards, the impoundment program does not transfer to the stream reaches above/below the dam.
A11. Other Beech River Watershed Development Authority lakes: Wikipedia lists the TVA "Beech River Project" (mid-20th c.) impounding the river and its seven major tributaries — Beech, Cedar, Dogwood, Lost Creek (no permanent reservoir), Pin Oak, Pine, Redbud, Sycamore; flood control/recreation only; some lakes lie within Natchez Trace State Park. TWRA datasets: NO trout rows for ANY of them — Decatur Co has zero trout sites in both the 2026 schedule and the 2016 layer. Their published regulations are warmwater: 2021-22 guide "Small Impoundments — Beech River Watershed Lakes: Catfish 5 per day, 15 inch minimum; Crappie 30 per day, no length limit; Pine Lake: Largemouth Bass 10 per day; Dogwood Lake: Largemouth 12- to 16-inch PLR, 10 per day; Redbud Lake: Largemouth 12- to 16-inch PLR, 10 per day" — no trout regulation, no trout mention. Confidence HIGH.

### Bottom line Q1
No trout stocking site, schedule row, plan mention, guide mention, or occurrence record exists for the Beech RIVER in any reach or season, current or (2016, 2021) historical. The entire watershed trout program is the Beech Lake winter-rainbow release (~1,500/yr, 2021 and 2026 documented), on the lake impoundment at Lexington — correctly EXCLUDED from the stream per standards. No other watershed lake is trout-stocked per TWRA's own datasets. Trout call: ABSENT on the river (weighted negative).

## RECOMMENDATION

**WARMWATER-FOCUS for the Beech River — upgrade from "unresolved"; the trout call for the watershed belongs exclusively to Beech Lake (seasonal-stocked lake program).**
Reasoning: (1) Zero trout rows for the river across the 616-row 2026 schedule, the 730-site current master layer, the 796-site 2016 layer, and the 2021-22 guide's stocking sections — while the SAME schedule/layer/guide documents the Beech Lake winter program (1,500 rainbows/yr, County-managed, listed in both 2021 and 2026) at the impoundment of the river itself. The river/lake distinction is exactly the ledger's concern and is now fully documented from TWRA's own data. No other BRWDA lake (Pine, Redbud, Dogwood, Cedar, Sycamore, Pin Oak) has any trout row; their published regs are catfish/crappie/largemouth. (2) The river's own documentation is warmwater and dated: the 2014 state proclamation opens the Beech River embayment (mouth to Hwy 69) to year-round commercial fishing with ALL gear (a designation trout-managed waters never carry); the 2021 guide regulates the watershed lakes' catfish/crappie/bass fisheries and jug-lining; TWRA owns and operates the Perryville ramp on "Kentucky Reservoir" waters and the ~4,886-ac Beech River WMA along the river; USGS gauged the river 1953-63 and 2010-24. (3) Independent occurrence data: GBIF Salmonidae = 0 in the watershed polygon vs 23,923 largemouth-bass records in the identical polygon — with the honest caveat that even the stocked Beech Lake rainbows are invisible to GBIF, so put-and-take blindness is demonstrated for this exact polygon. iNat has zero fish observations in the box (under-sampling); NAS blocked.
Key gaps: no method-documented fish species list for the river (TDEC sampling was chemistry-only 2008-09; no NRSA/TVA fish file located); the Beech River WMA page text (ledger's suggested next source) was unreachable — its fishing content remains unchecked; angler catch records unverified (search-engine outage); NAS county records unverified. Suggest labeling: "warmwater (Kentucky Lake embayment tributary; year-round commercial fishery mouth-to-Hwy 69 per 2014 proclamation; TWRA ramp + WMA; no trout evidence on any river reach; trout program in this watershed = Beech Lake winter rainbow stocking, lake-only, does not transfer to the stream)."
