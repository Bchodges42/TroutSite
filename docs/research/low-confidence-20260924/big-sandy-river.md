# Big Sandy River (Northwest Tennessee) — Trout-Occurrence Evidence Research Log

Water: Big Sandy River, northwest TN — 60-mi (97 km) free-flowing (channelized) tributary of the Tennessee River / Kentucky Lake; main stem rises ~5 mi NW of Lexington, Henderson Co, flows N through Carroll Co (Yuma, Westport, Bruceton), forms the Henry/Benton line, passes the town of Big Sandy (Benton Co), and enters Kentucky Lake as the biggest single embayment on the reservoir. Ledger state: limited / warmwater-focus (embayment managed by TWRA as warmwater: crappie/bass/catfish; dewatering-unit closures).
Research date: 2026-09-24 (all retrieval dates = 2026-09-24).
Researcher constraint: research only; no agency/business/author contact; no git writes; no writes except this file.

## GEOGRAPHY / FORK STRUCTURE (resolves the "?" in the task)

There is NO "East Sandy" fork and the river is NOT "formed by West Sandy Creek." West Sandy Creek is a large WESTERN TRIBUTARY ARM entering the lower river inside the impounded zone:
- Wikipedia, "Big Sandy River (Tennessee)" (retrieved 2026-09-24, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Big_Sandy_River_(Tennessee)): "The Big Sandy rises about 5 miles (8 km) northwest of Lexington, in the same vicinity as two other important rivers of West Tennessee, the Beech River and the Middle Fork of the Forked Deer River"; crosses into Carroll Co "near Yuma, passes east of Bruceton"; pre-channelization channel "forms several miles of the boundary between Benton County and Henry County"; "it forms the biggest single embayment on Kentucky Lake"; "The embayment's head is at the town of Big Sandy." West Sandy Creek = tributary whose "auxiliary dam" maintains a large dewatering area. Confidence HIGH (tertiary but consistent with agency data below). Not established: any trout content (none claimed).

### Bottom line Q1
No trout occurrence, stocking site, schedule row, plan mention, or citizen-science observation exists for ANY reach of the Big Sandy River (riverine or embayment). The only trout stocked in the three watershed counties are Winter-program rainbow releases into two urban park lakes (Paris City Park Lake, Henry Co; McKenzie City Park Lake, Carroll Co) — impoundments, not the river. Whether the Paris park lake is even hydrologically in the Big Sandy basin is unverified (Paris sits near the West Sandy/Tennessee divide); it does not affect the river call either way. Trout call: ABSENT (weighted negative), all reaches, all seasons, no historical planting paper trail located either (archived 2003–2026 schedules carry no Sandy-named water; 2003 PDF is a scanned image and not text-extractable, but its 2026-successor JSON plus the site layer plus the 60-pp plan close the loop).

## QUESTION 4 — WHERE KENTUCKY LAKE BACKWATER ENDS (reach definition)

Convergent evidence places the head of the embayment (upper limit of Kentucky Lake backwater) at ≈ Big Sandy River RM 15, at the town of Big Sandy (36.23–36.25 N, −88.10 W):
- TDEC WQP station names (A7): TDECWPC-BSAND015.1BN (36.248, −88.105) is officially named "BIG SANDY EMBAYMENT" while BSAND015.3BN (36.2325, −88.1003) and everything downstream to BSAND012.5BN is "BIG SANDY RIVER"/"BIG SANDY RV" — the EMBAYMENT label begins ≈RM 15 and runs down; the RIVER label resumes above RM 15.3. TDEC river miles count from the mouth at the Tennessee River.
- Wikipedia (geography section): "The embayment's head is at the town of Big Sandy."
- TVA (B2): "Big Sandy embayment" is one of four Kentucky Reservoir monitoring locations — i.e., reservoir water.
- TWRA Boat_Launch_Sites (C3): the town-of-Big Sandy ramp is waterway-labeled "Kentucky Reservoir."
- TWRA RiverMiles layer maps "Big Sandy River" miles 0–9 only (0 = Tennessee River confluence 36.4358/−88.0545; 9 = 36.3193/−88.1016 ≈ West Sandy confluence) — TWRA's mile 0–9 = lower embayment; numbering is consistent with TDEC's (RM ≈ 15.1 at lat 36.248 by linear interpolation of mile 0→9 markers).
- NWIS gauge 03607000 "BIG SANDY R AT BIG SANDY TENN" sits at the town (36.23061/−88.10448) with gauge datum 342.85 ft NGVD29 — below Kentucky Lake summer pool (359 ft) elevation context; consistent with backwater reaching at least to the town gauge.
- Upstream of ≈RM 15 the water is the free-flowing (channelized) river: TDEC 303(d) riverine segments (032-1000 Carroll 7.3 mi; 032-2000 Carroll/Henderson 12.5 mi), NRSA site TN-10149 (≈RM 41), USGS gauges Yuma/Westport/Bruceton/Wildersville (RMs ≈ 41–56 by the BSAND series: BSAND056.0HE at 35.7383/−88.4158 headwaters; BSAND049.2HE at 35.7935/−88.3467 ≈ Wildersville reach; BSAND036.4CR 35.957/−88.2844 ≈ Bruceton; BSAND029.7CR 36.0422/−88.2364 ≈ NARS lat).
So: EMBAYMENT (Kentucky Reservoir; warmwater; TWRA-developed access; TVA-monitored; dewatering units incl. West Sandy arm + Springville Bottom) = mouth to ≈RM 15 at town of Big Sandy. RIVERINE (free-flowing channelized river, no TWRA trout or access development) = ≈RM 15 up to headwaters ≈RM 56 near Wildersville. Classification should keep the split explicit.

## RECOMMENDATION

**WARMWATER-FOCUS — keep, with confidence raised from "limited" and the reach split made explicit.**
Reasoning: (1) The strongest possible agency negatives stack up for trout: zero Sandy rows in the 616-row 2026 schedule; zero trout sites on any reach in the ~700-site master layer (only park-lake sites in two watershed counties, both Winter-rainbow urban impoundments); zero mentions in the 60-page statewide trout plan; zero Salmonidae in GBIF (0) and iNat (0/774 TN obs) across the whole watershed polygon while the same polygon yields bass/etc. (16 M. salmoides). No historical plant located either (2003–2026 schedule archive + plan). (2) Warmwater documentation for the system is dated and official: TDEC bass-mercury advisory for the river+embayment (tissue collections 2009–2016+, crappie/catfish explicitly present), 2000 commercial-fishery rule language, TVA fish-community monitoring at the embayment location, NRSA benthic sample 2019, TDEC 303(d) assessments. (3) Reach definition is now pinned: Kentucky Lake backwater ends ≈RM 15 at the town of Big Sandy (TDEC "EMBAYMENT" station label, TVA monitoring location, TWRA "Kentucky Reservoir" ramp label, Wikipedia embayment head); everything above (≈RM 15–56, Wildersville→Yuma→Westport→Bruceton→town) is the free-flowing riverine reach with no trout history and no TWRA-developed fishery infrastructure. Not "unresolved": the negatives are unusually broad and multi-source. Not "seasonal-stocked": the only seasonal trout in the counties are park lakes off the river.
Key gap: no method-documented, published fish SPECIES LIST for the free-flowing reach itself (NRSA/TDEC sampling was benthic/chemistry; TVA's list is for the embayment and unpublished at site level). Likely record holder: TDEC DWR fish-tissue program field records (bass/crappie/catfish collected from the river 2009–2016) and TWRA Region 1 (West TN) fisheries files; secondary: Etnier & Starnes (1993) voucher-based locality records. Suggest labeling the free-flowing reach "warmwater (documented bass/crappie/catfish via tissue advisory; no trout evidence, any category)".
