# Smokies access pilot — official-source review, October 1, 2026

Two parking records are associated with existing catalog waters. This is a desk
review of official land-manager pages, not an in-person access or safety survey.
Coordinates, fees and accessible river entry are unconfirmed and omitted.
These records are not inferred from stocking markers.

| Record | Source and checked claims | Limits |
|---|---|---|
| `little-river-metcalf-bottoms-parking` | [NPS Metcalf Bottoms](https://www.nps.gov/places/metcalf-bottoms-picnic-area.htm) lists “Parking - Auto,” 122 picnic sites along Little River, a fishing-license requirement, a 10-ton bridge limit, no buses/motorhomes/trailers, and winter dumpster removal. | Current parking rules, closures and bank routes need a departure-time check. The page's last-updated date is January 11, 2025; retrieval in 2026 does not make that an on-site observation. |
| `west-prong-little-pigeon-chimneys-parking` | [NPS Chimneys](https://www.nps.gov/places/chimneys-picnic-area.htm) lists “Parking - Auto,” 68 sites beside West Prong Little Pigeon on Highway 441, seasonal sunrise opening, evening closing times, and a steep loop trail. | The page describes operation from early April to December 1; exact annual and temporary closures need checking. Its last-updated date is August 15, 2026. |
