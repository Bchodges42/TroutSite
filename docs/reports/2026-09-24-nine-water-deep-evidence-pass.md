- **Standards:** broad method-documented fish surveys with full species lists omitting trout count as meaningful negative evidence, weighted by site number/distribution, dates/seasons, method, effort, list completeness, and reach match; non-detection ≠ literal absence and old surveys ≠ current conditions. Fishbrain material is internal-research-only and never proposed for production.

### 1. Elk River, Prospect → Tennessee–Alabama state line — recommendation: **warmwater-focus**

This resolves the earlier ambiguity: the GIS layer's "Old Dam Ford — Winter — rainbow" site sits on the tailwater end, **not** on the catalog reach. Establishes that no mapped trout water extends onto the Prospect→state-line stem; does not by itself prove no historical stocking.

This **contradicts** the GIS layer's Winter tagging at Old Dam Ford and is flagged as a TWRA-side question; it does not change the catalog-reach conclusion either way.
- **TWRA Region IV smallmouth study:** Spaulding & Rogers 2020, *J. SE Assoc. Fish & Wildlife Agencies* 7:33–40 (`https://seafwa.org/journal/2020/smallmouth-bass-population-characteristics-and-minimum-length-limit-evaluation-two`) — TWRA boat-electrofishing of Elk River (+Richland Creek) smallmouth, May–June 2018: agency survey treating the river as a smallmouth fishery.
- **Harms Mill Dam removal (lower-river fish life):** USFWS project page `https://www.fws.gov/project/harms-mill-dam-removal` cites 46 species of greatest conservation need plus ~139 other fish species associated with the reach; removal completed October 2025; WPLN's 2025-10-20 report (`https://wpln.org/post/shock-conservation-keeping-the-boulder-darter-out-of-harms-way/`) documents Conservation Fisheries + TWRA electrofishing relocations. - **Occurrences:** iNaturalist rainbow observations in the corridor (23) all sit at the tailwater sites (HWY 50 / Farris Creek / off-river); GBIF returns zero reach trout; USGS NAS has a single 1992 "Elk River, Fayetteville, TN" rainbow with status "stocked" (Boschung 1992 catalog) — one-catch-rule lead only.
- **Limits/gaps:** no completed-release records for Old Dam Ford located (TWRA TSAS 1990–2016 coldwater export and Region 2 winter logs would settle it); the concurrent session's Geological Survey of Alabama 2004 survey (two Giles County main-stem stations, 91-species system list, no trout) adds strong border-end negative evidence and points to Jandebeur's 1972 whole-drainage thesis as an unread older source.

### 2. East Fork Stones River — recommendation: **warmwater-focus**

- **MTSU thesis (strongest modern source):** Williams 2022, *Assessing the Linkage Between Aquatic Biodiversity and Water Chemistry in the Stones River Watershed* (`https://jewlscholar.mtsu.edu/handle/mtsu/6850`). eDNA sampling at three East Fork sites (Goochie Ford, Walter Hill Dam, Woodbury bridge) twice each, Aug–Nov 2021: 150 ray-finned-fish ASVs across 13 families/33 genera (Campostoma, Lepomis, Etheostoma, Pimephales, Hypentelium, Moxostoma dominant); East Fork richness exceeded the West Fork; **no trout taxon appears anywhere**. Method-documented, multi-site, current-decade.
- **Museum collections 1963–2023 (GBIF pulls):** NCSM (1967/2000/2009), UAIC (1963/1968/1970/1990/1991/1996, e.g. "just below dam" US 231, 1990), Bell/UMich-Simons (1999–2007, Walter Hill Dam + US 231), Auburn (1975), OSUM (1964/1986), APSU (1982; 2016-05-13 "just below Walter Hill Dam"), Yale Peabody (2023) — ~50+ species across ≥8 sites spanning the reach; **zero salmonids in six decades** of vouchered sampling.
- **Species-level occurrence checks:** GBIF Oncorhynchus mykiss in the corridor = 4 records, all artifacts (one is a West Fork/Smyrna iNat point, others bbox-edge museum rows from other drainages); Salmo trutta = 1 unusable (state-non-specific); Salvelinus fontinalis = 0. iNaturalist: 544 fish observations in the corridor including 2025–2026, all warmwater.
- **TDEC data is warmwater and targeted:** WQP fish rows at East Fork stations (TNW000002137/2138/7455/8306 in 2018; TNW000008453 in 2022) are single-species tissue collections (largemouth/smallmouth/rock bass, channel catfish); TDEC's 2019 precautionary mercury advisory (mouth at Percy Priest upstream to Betty Ford Road) frames the river as a warmwater fishery. SQKICK benthic runs continue through 2026.
- **TWRA program check (live page, agent-verified):** only "W. Fork Stones River – Manson Pike Trailhead" and "J. Percy Priest Dam, Stones River" (rainbow, Dec–Mar) exist; nothing on the East Fork. Community sources (realisticfishing.com 2023-07-22 below Walter Hill: longear/bluegill/green sunfish; a 2026-09-08 trip report: "large number and diversity of fish," no trout) are uniformly warmwater.
- **Limits/gaps:** the TSAS 1990–2016 export would make "no stocking ever" definitive (TWRA Region 2, Nashville); USACE's Percy Priest pre-impoundment report and the print copy of Mullen's 2006 NPS inventory remain unlocated.
- **Unproductive lanes:** USGS NAS API (403); Wayback CDX domain filter (504s); old TDEC Stones WQMP PDFs not archived; FERC eLibrary unreachable (Walter Hill dam inactive since 1941).

### 4. Pigeon River, Tennessee reach — recommendation: **warmwater-focus**, with a documented **historical experimental trout program on the upper gorge** (Walters Powerhouse → Bluffton, ~PRM 19–26), ca. Dec 2009–2015

- **TWRA Region IV Warmwater Streams Report 2010 (lead-verified verbatim from the Wayback PDF, Report No. 11-03):** "In December of 2009, an experimental stocking of fingerling rainbow trout was accomplished between river mile 19 and 26. The experimental release of **41,793** (100-125 mm) rainbow trout was done to evaluate the possibility of…"; a further "40,000 rainbow trout in 2010"; sport-fish surveys at five sites "between Newport and Walters Powerhouse"; highest smallmouth catches at site 4 (Bluffton). The fleet's reads of the 2010/2011 volumes record 2 rainbows (Jul 2010) and 6 rainbows (2011) in IBIs at Denton (PRM 16.6), and 2014/2015 recommendations to "continue stocking… between the powerhouse and Bluffton with rainbow trout when available."
- **The program self-terminated:** complete species tables in the 2012–2017 volumes (Tannery Island PRM 8.2 and Denton PRM 16.6 IBIs since 1988; CPUE at 5 sites PRM 4.0–20.5 since 1997; 24–39 species lists) contain **zero trout**, and the 2016–2017 recommendations drop trout entirely in favor of native warmwater/musky recovery. The TWRA *Region IV Coldwater Streams 2020* report (Wayback, 77 pp) has zero Pigeon/Waterville/Hartford/Cocke mentions; 2010–2018 stocking schedules list Cocke destinations as tributaries only (Gulf Fork Big Creek, Cosby Creek, Brush Creek, Trail Fork Big Creek).
- **Occurrence context:** USGS NAS Cocke County holds 4 brown-trout records attributed to "TWRA Fish Data via BISON," 1997–2004, with rounded centroids near the upper gorge — coarse precision, pre-dating the stocking program, cannot be placed on the main stem. iNaturalist's Sep 2026 mouth-of-river assemblage at Newport (11 species) has no trout; GBIF has none; TDEC's Pigeon work is macroinvertebrate-only and the dioxin dataset (1996–2002) is tissue-only. One-catch lead: an easttennesseefishing.com thread (14 Jul 2010) reporting "~40 fish … and 1 Rainbow Trout" — squarely inside the experimental-stocking window, reach unconfirmed.
- **Reading:** the strongest trout-positive agency evidence found for any of the nine waters, and simultaneously evidence that TWRA itself treated the reach as warmwater before, during (experimental), and after. The 2025 walleye release + "minimal fish-population changes" statement is consistent with the warmwater rotation.
- **Limits/gaps:** the 2018–2024 Pigeon reports and the specific "recent survey" behind the May 2025 walleye statement are unpublished — held by TWRA Region IV (Fall Branch) and the fisheries chief's office (Nashville).
- **Unproductive lanes:** Etnier & Starnes full text unreachable; BISON API retired; FishNet2 JS app; TDEC dioxin PDF direct path dead; pre-2010 raw IBI data not posted.

### 5. Powell River, Tennessee main stem — recommendation: **warmwater-focus**

- **TWRA's own main-stem survey series (decisive):** Region IV Warmwater Streams Reports carry full Powell River chapters (2008 report pp. 17–23; 2017 report pp. 44–51; Wayback URLs above): 8 boat-electrofishing sites from the VA line (RM 115) down to RM 75 (sites defined to RM 59 near the Norris backwater), 3-year rotation 1999/2002/2005/2008/2011/2014/2017. TWRA's own words: "one of the premier warmwater rivers in east Tennessee… one of east Tennessee's better warmwater sport fisheries." Smallmouth (CPUE ~50/hr) and rock bass are the entire sportfish story; **no trout stocking, regulation, or observation is ever mentioned** — while the same report series explicitly documents trout stockings on the Nolichucky and Little River, i.e., TWRA records trout where they exist.
- **Museum record:** GBIF/UMMZ main-stem records 1890 ("8 mi S of Cumberland Gap"), 1936, and 1939-09-08 (US 25E near Harrogate, ~28 taxa incl. slender chub) — zero salmonids across ~96 records spanning 130 years; a single 1937 UMMZ rainbow (104215) is from Little Sycamore Creek, a **tributary** — watershed-level lead only.
- **The one modern "trout in the river" claim resolves to a stocked tributary:** iNat 346568709 (2025-10-21, research grade, 4–5 rainbows photographed) at "Colwyn St, Cumberland Gap TN" plots on **Gap Creek** — the town creek, ~470 m from the main stem — with TWRA's spring-rainbow stocking points on the same creek ~2 km downstream. All other trout in the polygon are Norris Lake/tailwater or VA headwaters (NAS HUC 06010206: 3 trout records, all VA 1994).
- **Agency frameworks:** TDEC's 2007 Powell watershed plan (Wayback) and 2008 E. coli TMDL contain no trout designations; TVA has run 9 IBI fish-assemblage sites on the Powell since 2000; NPS's 2004 park fish inventory (22 species) found no trout; the only WQP fish taxa on the main stem (2011 tissue, TNW000004941) are smallmouth and rock bass.
- **Limits/gaps:** TVA's station-level Powell IBI lists are not public (TVA stream monitoring; a contact, Charlie Saylor, appears in the TDEC 2007 plan); TWRA Region IV (Morristown) holds pre-1999 surveys (Bivens 1988; Carter et al. 2000–2006); the 1968 TVA "Tennessee Valley Streams: Powell River drainage basin" (Google Books id BjYlAQAAMAAJ) could not be downloaded.
- **Unproductive lanes:** HathiTrust/Google Books full text (auth-walled); SEFC Simmons 2021 full text; EPA ERA PDF (NEPIS blocked); legacy HUC 05010106 superseded by 06010206 in modern systems.
