## Scope and method

- **Set derivation:** the ledger's own weakness signal is `headline.evidenceState`: 138 documented vs **18 limited + 3 conflicting + 31 unresolved = 52 waters**. Five of the 52 were completed earlier the same day by the nine-water pass (elk-river-lower, east-fork-stones-river, new-river, reedy-creek, south-fork-cumberland); this pass covers the remaining **47**.
- **Method:** 41 waters via 21 web-research subagents (6 solo complex waters; 15 paired by drainage; each covering five lanes: agency datasets, academic/museum, occurrence repositories, local/community, historical/physical), **6 waters by the lead session directly** after a provider 5-hour quota killed three agents mid-wave (dumplin, little-chuckey, crab-orchard partial, roaring, west-fork-obey; the quota-killed Brimstone agent finished its log before dying). One research log per water under `docs/research/low-confidence-20260924/` (47 files), each with per-source fields, reach discipline, contradictions, searches including unproductive lanes, and a recommendation.
- **Standards:** identical to the nine-water pass — broad method-documented full-list surveys omitting trout are meaningful negative evidence (weighted by sites/distribution, dates/seasons, method, effort, list completeness, reach match); non-detection ≠ literal absence; single catches are leads (owner's one-catch rule); schedules ≠ completed releases ≠ holdover; regulatory labels ≠ presence; Fishbrain internal-research-only.

## Systemic findings (beyond individual waters)

1. **Fishbrain water-page "trout" boilerplate identified — invalidates several `fishbrain-trout-lead` flags.** Fishbrain's public water pages append a site-wide 20-species index that is verbatim identical on every water page (it even lists red drum, a marine fish, and "brown trout" appeared on North Mouse Creek's page *and* on a Missouri control page). Several ledger fishbrain-trout-lead flags plausibly originate from this boilerplate rather than any actual catch. North Mouse's "brown trout lead" is downgraded to non-evidence. **The other fishbrain-trout-lead flags in the ledger (nolichucky, mississippi, nonconnah, north-fork-forked-deer, north-mouse, ocoee, roaring) should be re-derived against actual dated catches, not page-level species indices.**
2. **onWater/PaddleWays auto-generated "trout" claims are noise.** The same content-farm pattern asserted rainbow trout on Brimstone Creek, Clear Fork, and the West Fork Obey — each contradicted by every primary dataset. Treat aggregator trout claims as non-evidence across the board.
3. **A reusable TWRA archive corpus now exists:** the Region IV Warmwater Streams report series 1986–2017 (digitalcommons.memphis.edu + Wayback pattern), annual stocked-trout schedules 2003–2015 (state.tn.us `sched03–15.pdf` era) and 2018–2025 (tn.gov `Complete.pdf` captures), winter/coldwater reports 2010–2023, and the machine-readable 2026 schedule JSON (616 rows). Archive hazard caught this pass: seven "different" 2021–25 `Trout-Stocking-Schedule-Complete.pdf` Wayback captures are byte-identical replays of the 2020 file — md5 any capture before citing it as year-specific.
4. **HUC/identity corrections:** Big Swan Creek is Lewis + **Hickman** (not Lewis/Maury); GNIS has no plain "Bigby Creek" (only Big Bigby / Little Bigby); "Chestua" is historical spelling only; "Bullrun" vs "Bull Run" both live; the Forked Deer main stem is the Dyer/Lauderdale 14.9-mi segment (Middle Fork joins the North Fork, not the South); Dickinson 1973 is the whole-Obion-system anchor.
5. **Museum-lane wins:** vouchered collections now anchor five previously evidence-empty waters (Little Chucky 1993; West Fork Obey 1975 incl. the endemic *Etheostoma obeyense*; Brimstone 1891/1893/1953/1968/2007; Big Bigby 1928–2023; Big Swan 1937–2022) — all trout-free. iDigBio/GBIF museum queries should be a standing lane for "empty" waters.

## Per-water recommendations (47)

**Seasonal-stocked (6):**
- **shoal-creek** — conflict resolved: TWRA's schedule rows named "East Fork Shoal Creek" are a Lawrence County tributary through Lawrenceburg (the Dickson-Co attribution was a phantom; NHD shows zero Shoal waters in Dickson). Documented program 2003–2026 (archived schedules 2003–08, 2018–20, completed releases 1/2/2019, 1/8/2020, 5/14/2024; 2026 live rows), cold-season put-and-take, TWRA GIS main-stem site "Davy Crockett State Park" on Shoal Creek proper (35.2636, -87.3566) with research-grade iNat rainbows 133 m away. Gaps: 2009–17 continuity.
- **nolichucky-river** — strongest surprise: TWRA's own Region IV reports (2011, 2017) state verbatim that Erwin NFH stocks the upper Nolichucky with rainbow trout in winter, and a dated USFWS photo documents a 3/14/2022 broodstock release on the river. Never a scheduled TWRA program (zero rows anywhere); warmwater smallmouth management dominates. Gaps: post-Helene status (TWRA Region 4 + Erwin NFH hold the ledgers).
- **paris-city-park-lake** — identity settled in the owner's favor: TWRA's GIS point (36.28656, -88.30512) sits inside the Eiffel Tower Park block (park centroid 36.28702, -88.30560), while GNIS "Green Acres Lake" is 2.57 km away and GNIS has no "Williams Lake" in Henry County. Winter program documented since ≥2010-11. Bucket: seasonal-stocked (winter put-and-take).
- **north-prong-barren-fork** — spring-only (Mar–May) in all 23 checked schedule years (2003–2026); the catalog's Dec–Feb winter claim is contradicted in every year. Completed releases 5/16/2018, 3/31/2020. Site: Petigap Rd Bridge S1 (35.6977, -85.9560), 800 rainbow/event, private land. No holdover evidence.
- **fort-patrick-henry-lake** — the year-round test is not met by data: no dated lake schedule row in any year checked (all "Patrick Henry" rows are the tailwater), but the Region IV Coldwater Report 2018 documents the two-story-reservoir program ("adult Rainbow Trout during the late fall and winter," put-and-take on the reservoir since 1978) and two Reservoir-program GIS sites exist (incl. Warrior's Path SP). Owner policy: continuous stocking alone defines year-round; non-winter stockings unproven → seasonal-stocked, upgrade path exists via Region 4 logs. A 2024 state-record cutthroat (6 lb 9 oz, "Boone tailwater" label) is an upper-arm occurrence datum.
- **parksville-tailwater** — program verified (rainbow Mar–May; shortest statewide tailwater, ~3.4 mi; site 35.0957, -84.6514); holdover question resolves negative-leaning: no post-May catch/survey evidence anywhere, summer grabs 21.6–25.3 °C (2001–08) marginal-to-fatal for rainbows. Seasonal-stocked stands.
