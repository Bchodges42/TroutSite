### PASS1-2 — Portal proxy buffers request bodies before authentication

**Severity:** P1 · **Confidence:** verified · **Area:** availability/security  
**Location:** [infra/static-server.mjs:85](/Users/ben/Downloads/TroutSite-review-20260911/infra/static-server.mjs:85).
